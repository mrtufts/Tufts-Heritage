Names Under Research

Some accounts name these men as Revolutionary-era Tufts. They are not yet confirmed here, and none was found among the Tufts entries of Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors, vol. XVI, pp. 130–140.

Ichabod Tufts (said to be killed at Bunker Hill, 1775); Elias Tufts (said to have served in the 15th Massachusetts); Frederick Tufts (said to be a sergeant of colonial militia); James Tufts (1726–1786) (supporter of the cause). Each needs a primary record.
