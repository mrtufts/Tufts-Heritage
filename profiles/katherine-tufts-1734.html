Biography

Katherine (also Catherine, and Catharine in Wyman) Tufts was born on 31 March 1734, the eldest child of Lieut. William Tufts of Medford by his first wife, Catharine Wyman (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 961. Page (Internet Archive)).

On 10 January 1750 she married her cousin William Tufts Jr., a mariner of Medford, son of John Tufts. He died on 25 May 1771, aged 44, “left widow and children” (Essex Gazette, as cited by Wyman). She married, second, Nathaniel Wyman in 1772, and third, Eben Richardson in 1776 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 961. Page (Internet Archive)).

Among her children by William were Ebenezer Tufts (1761–1830), William Tufts (1762–1777), who was killed in the Revolutionary service, and Eliakim Tufts (1767–1825). Wild records William as “Son of William and Catherine (Tufts); born 1762; killed 1777.”

Service Record

Town: Medford, Mass. · Dates: 31 March 1734 – [death date not found]

No military service is recorded for her. Her son William served and was killed in 1777; Ebenezer was a Revolutionary pensioner.
