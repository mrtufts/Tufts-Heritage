Biography

The name Gardner Tufts is known here from the docket of a cotton-trade letter or invoice of 1811. The photograph shows only the wrapper, inscribed “Gardner Tufts / Cotton 1811.” The letter itself has not been read.

He is not Col. Gardiner Tufts of Lynn, born 3 July 1828, who was not yet living in 1811. A merchant Gardner Tufts, born at Brookfield, Massachusetts, on 15 July 1777, later of Savannah, and died at sea at St. Johns, Antigua, on 18 February 1824, was then living and in trade (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 94, no. 45-4). That he is the man named on this docket has not been shown.
