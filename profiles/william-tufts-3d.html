Biography

William Tufts, styled 3d, was born at Medford on January 11, 1758, the son of William and Rebeccah 3d: “Tufts, William, s. William and Rebeccah 3d, Jan. 11, 1758.” Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston, 1907), p. 153. An older brother of the same name had been born May 20, 1756, and died February 12, 1757: “William, s. William and Rebeccah 3d, Feb. 12, 1757” (same volume, p. 446; birth on p. 153).

He died at Medford on January 4, 1782, aged 24, as recorded from a gravestone: “William 3d, Jan. 4, 1782, a. 24. g.r.i.” Medford VR, p. 446. From the January 11, 1758, birth that age is 23 years, 11 months, and 24 days, consistent with a recorded age of 24.

Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 71, no. 31-3, identifies him as the son of William Tufts Jr. (family 11-3; d. 24 Oct. 1775 at Medford, a. 45, “Bror to ye Doctor,” Medford VR, p. 446) and Rebecca Tufts 3rd, and says he died unmarried. Kinsmen prints the death as 4 January 1784, citing the same Medford VR page; that year conflicts with the printed vital record and gravestone, which read 1782. This page follows the vital record.

He is not William Tufts (1736–1782), a different William of Medford whose published dates (born 1736; died 27 April 1782) cannot be reconciled with a death at age 24.

Service Record

Town: Medford, Mass. · Dates: 11 Jan. 1758 – 4 Jan. 1782

No entry under a distinctive “William 3d” style was found in Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War, vol. XVI. The Medford William entries in that volume are already associated with other men on this site and are not assigned to him here.

Family Connection

Parents: William Tufts Jr. (d. 24 Oct. 1775, Medford VR, p. 446) and Rebecca (“Rebeccah 3d”) Tufts. Infant brother: William, b. 20 May 1756, d. 12 Feb. 1757 (Medford VR, pp. 153, 446). Grandfather (compiled): Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747). Uncles: Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815), Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786), and Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799) (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, families 11-3 and 31, p. 71).

Relatives with profiles on this site: a different man: William Tufts (1736–1782); uncle Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815); uncle Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786); uncle Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799); grandfather (compiled) Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747).

Spouse(s)

No marriage has been found for him in the Medford vital records or in Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 71, which treats him as unmarried.
