Biography

Two entries under the name Zachariah Tufts of Woburn appear in Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors. The first follows a private in Capt. John Wood's company through 1775, in camp at Medford and Cambridge. The second is a 1780 descriptive list of a man aged 21, engaged for Woburn for three years.

Town: Woburn · Dates: [dates not in sources seen]

“TUFTS, ZACHARIAH, Woburn. List of men belonging to Capt. John Wood's co. who took the oath in Middlesex Co. May 27, 1775, required by Congress to be taken by the Mass. army; also, Capt. Wood's (5th) co., Col. Samuel Gerrish's regt.; order for advance pay, signed by said Tufts and others, dated Cambridge, June 1, 1775; also, Private, Capt. Wood's co., Lieut. Col. Loammi Baldwin's (late Col. Gerrish's) 38th (also given 37th) regt.; muster roll dated Aug. 1, 1775; enlisted May 5, 1775; service, 88 days; …”

Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War, vol. XVI (1907), p. 140 · page scan

“TUFTS, ZACHARIAH. Descriptive list of men raised in Middlesex Co., agreeable to resolve of Dec. 2, 1780, as returned by Joseph Hosmer, Superintendent for said county; age, 21 yrs.; stature, 5 ft. 9 in.; complexion, light; hair, light; eyes, blue; occupation, laborer (also given farmer); engaged for town of Woburn; engaged April 1, 1781; term, 3 years.”

Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War, vol. XVI (1907), p. 140 · page scan
