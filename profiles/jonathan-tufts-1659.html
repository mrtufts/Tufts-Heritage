Biography

Jonathan Tufts was a son of Peter Tufts the immigrant and Mary (Pierce) Tufts, and a brother of Capt. Peter Tufts and of John Tufts, the father of Nathaniel Tufts. Wyman places him in Malden and Medford, and records his death on 13 August 1722, aged 63 years, 3 months and 11 days, with a gravestone at Malden, which would place his birth at about 2 May 1659 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 961. Page (Internet Archive)).

He married Rebecca Wait. Wyman lists nine children: Jonathan (1685–1688); James; John (b. 1688); Jonathan (b. 6 February 1690); Rebecca (b. 1694, m. John Willis); Samuel (b. 29 April 1697); Persis (b. 1700); Joseph (b. 1704); and Abigail (1707–1726) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 961. Page (Internet Archive)).

A 1931 account of an early Medford town meeting names a Jonathan Tufts as tithing man, with Capt. Peter Tufts as moderator and selectman (Wilson Fiske and Ruth Dame Coolidge, “The Old Time Medford Town Meeting,” Medford Historical Register XXXIV, no. 4 (December 1931), p. 44). The identification of that officer with this Jonathan, Capt. Peter’s brother, is likely, but it is not confirmed in the account. The scene is on Capt. Peter Tufts’s profile.

Family Connection

Father: Peter Tufts the immigrant (1617–1700). Mother: Mary Pierce (d. 1702/3). Brothers include Capt. Peter Tufts (c. 1648–1721) and John Tufts (d. 1728), father of Nathaniel Tufts (1692–1741) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 961. Page (Internet Archive); Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Peter Tufts the immigrant; brother Capt. Peter Tufts (c. 1648–1721); nephew Nathaniel Tufts (1692–1741).

Spouse(s)
