Biography

Andrew Tufts served as a private with the Northern Army at Saratoga.

The Worcester Tanyard, 1800

“In 1789, Palmer and Daniel Goulding owned the Tanyard in Worcester. In 1800 it was in the hands of Andrew Tufts, and later owners were Samuel Johnson, Thomas Stearns, Asa Wilder & Co., Nymphas Pratt and Ebenezer H. Bowen.”

Franklin P. Rice, Dictionary of Worcester (Massachusetts) and Its Vicinity (1893), p. 57. Internet Archive (Transcribed from the printed page.)

⚑ Unverified

The Dictionary gives no further detail about this Andrew Tufts. That he is the Andrew Tufts of this page, who died at Worcester, is likely but not stated. Note: Pending further work.

Service Record

Town: Medford; Worcester · Dates: 1761 – 1836

Born 6 Jan. 1761 in Medford; died 20 Apr. 1836 in Worcester; private with the Northern Army at Saratoga, N.Y.; head of household 1790 and 1800. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 91, family no. 43-3)
