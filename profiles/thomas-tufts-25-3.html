Biography

Thomas Tufts of Lee, New Hampshire, served as a private and later as a town highway surveyor.

Service Record

Town: Lee, N.H. · Dates: 1757 – 1834

Born 1757; died 1834; highway surveyor; private in the Revolution. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 137, family no. 25-3; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Family Connection

Family number 25-3 in Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I. Parents are not named in the extract seen.

⚑ Unverified

Vol. I’s Henry entry says Henry followed “brother Thomas” to Newmarket; a Thomas born 1757 is unlikely to be that brother of a man born about 1735. Relationship not confirmed.
