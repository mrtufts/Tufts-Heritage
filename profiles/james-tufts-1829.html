Biography

James Tufts was born at Charlestown, New Hampshire, on September 19, 1829, a son of Jonas Tufts and Sarah (Labaree) Tufts. He graduated from Middlebury College in 1855 and was admitted to the bar at Burlington, Iowa, in 1857. In 1859 he moved to Niobrara in Nebraska Territory, where he was elected probate judge and sat in the territorial legislature (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 166, no. 309-3; Find a Grave; Duane Hamilton Hurd, ed., History of Cheshire and Sullivan Counties, New Hampshire (Philadelphia: J. W. Lewis, 1886), Alstead sketch of Timothy Tufts, transcribed at Access Genealogy (printed page not confirmed)).

In Dakota Territory he was secretary of the legislative council in 1861–62, and in 1863 he went to Idaho, where he served in the territorial House and was its first Speaker (Find a Grave). He was Secretary of Montana Territory from March 28, 1867, to April 19, 1869 (Montana Historical Society, Compass: Facts). With Gov. Green Clay Smith absent, the executive duties fell to him. The Interior Department later transmitted his account for the period June 10, 1868, to July 1, 1869, as “acting governor and ex-officio superintendent of Indian affairs” (letter of 16 Feb. 1871, U.S. Congressional Serial Set).

He was an unsuccessful candidate for Montana’s seat as delegate to Congress and afterward returned to Niobrara to farm and practice law. He died there on August 18, 1884, and is buried at L’Eau Qui Court Cemetery (Find a Grave). Hurd calls him the brother of Timothy Tufts of Alstead.

Service Record

Town: Charlestown, N.H.; Iowa; Nebraska; Dakota, Idaho and Montana Territories · Dates: 19 Sept. 1829 – 18 Aug. 1884

Public offices: probate judge and legislator in Nebraska Territory; secretary of the council in Dakota Territory; Speaker of the first Idaho territorial House; Secretary and acting governor of Montana Territory. No military service is recorded for him in the family record (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 166).

Family Connection

Parents: Jonas Tufts (1798–1879) and Sarah (Labaree) Tufts. Brother: Timothy Tufts of Alstead (1824–1901). Nephew: James Arthur Tufts (1855–1938).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Jonas Tufts (1798–1879); brother Timothy Tufts of Alstead (1824–1901); nephew James Arthur Tufts (1855–1938).

Spouse(s)

He did not marry (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 166).

Dates of his acting governorship differ by source: the Montana Historical Society lists summer 1868 to summer 1869, the Interior Department account runs from June 10, 1868, to July 1, 1869, and some compilations give only March–April 1869. The year of his run for delegate is 1869 in one source and 1870 in Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 166, which also misprints his Middlebury year as 1885 (Hurd and Find a Grave give 1855) and credits him with the first book on Montana, which has not been located here.
