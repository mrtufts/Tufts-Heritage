Biography

A few weeks after the death of Eliakim Tufts of Littleton (19 February 1825), the town recorded the intention of marriage of “Leonard Porter of Carlisle and Daranda Tufts of Littleton, May 1st 1825” (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 282. Internet Archive). The entry is quoted on Eliakim’s profile, which identifies her as his daughter Dorinda.

Service Record

Town: Littleton, Mass. · Dates: flourished 1825

No military service is recorded for her.

Family Connection

Father: Eliakim Tufts (1767–1825) of Medford, Billerica, Groton and Littleton. The compiled list of Eliakim’s children on his profile includes a daughter in this generation; the Littleton intention is the contemporary record of her name and residence in 1825.

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Eliakim Tufts (1767–1825).

Spouse(s)
