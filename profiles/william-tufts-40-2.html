Biography

William Tufts Jr. of Medford served as a private in a company of guards near Boston late in the war, and died in 1782.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: 1736 – 1782

Born 27 Mar. 1736 in Medford; died 27 Apr. 1782. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 158, family no. 40-2; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Private in Capt. Nathan Sargent’s Company of Guards, under Stephen Dana, Col. McIntosh’s Regiment, Massachusetts Line (the book cites DAR 92:120). (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 158, family no. 40-2; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

His brother Aaron (40-4) marched in the French War in 1757 for the relief of Fort William Henry and never returned. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 158, family no. 40-2; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)
