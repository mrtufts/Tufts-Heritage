Biography

Many records name a Samuel Tufts, most of them from Medford. Medford itself told its Samuels apart as Samuel, Samuel Jr., and Samuel 3d. Each record appears separately below. Matches with Wild's biographical notes are given only where the details agree closely.

Service Record

Town: Medford; Brookline; others not given · Dates: See entries

“TUFTS, SAMUEL, Brookline. Petition dated Camp at Hull, Sept. 17, 1776, signed by said Tufts and others belonging to battalion stationed at Hull, asking for increase and payment of wages; also, Private, Capt. William Warner's co., Col. Josiah Whitney's regt.; service from May 19, 1776, 4 days preceding march, to Nov. 1, 1776, 5 mos. 13 days; …”

Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War, vol. XVI (1907), p. 138 · page scan

“TUFTS, SAMUEL, Medford. Private, Capt. Isaac Hall's co., Col. Thomas Gardner's regt., which assembled April 19, 1775; service, 5 days.”

“TUFTS, SAMUEL (also given SAMUEL, Jr.), Medford. … Private, Capt. Family Connection

“TUFTS, SAMUEL. Private, Walton's Co. at Noddle's Island, 1776; wages paid to his father, Joseph Tufts. Born 1759 or 1760; his mother's name was Hannah; married Martha Upham; died in Westford, 1818 or 1819; occupation, dyer.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 52 (matches the MSS Noddle's Island entry: father Joseph Tufts) · page scan

“TUFTS, SAMUEL. Enlisted, 1780, for 6 mos.; age 19. Son of Samuel and Hannah (Tufts); born April, 1761; taxed 1781.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 52 · page scan

“TUFTS, SAMUEL, 3D. Private, Lexington alarm; paid money for bounty to men going to N. Y. (Called Jr., 1776.) Born 1752; son of William and Mary (Francis); married Margaret Hodgkins of Ipswich before 1779; died Nov. 20, 1815; gravestone in Salem Street Cemetery.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 53 · page scan

“TUFTS, SAMUEL. Private, Lexington alarm, loaned money to pay bounty July, 1776 (archives). Committee of safety, 1779 (town records). Son of Joseph and Lydia (Francis); born Aug. 16, 1732; married Hannah Tufts; died 1818; aged 86; gravestone, Salem Street Cemetery.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 52 · page scan

From Tufts Kinsmen

Vol. II traces several separate Samuel5 lines. These include “Samuel5, William4, James3, Jonathan2, Peter1” (p. 96), “Samuel5, Joseph4, Joseph3, Jonathan2, Peter1” (pp. 107–111), and “Samuel5, Samuel4, Joseph3, Jonathan2, Peter1” (p. 114). (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II)

Possible matches only. Wild’s Samuel 3d was the son of William, his Samuel b. 1732 the son of Joseph, and his Samuel b. 1761 the son of Samuel. These fit the three lines above in parentage, but Vol. II gives no service or dates for any Samuel5. None is attached to a specific MSS entry.

Vol. I

Samuel Tufts (15-3): born 16 Aug. 1732 in Medford; died 31 Dec. 1818; married Hannah Tufts; answered the Lexington alarm; Committee of Safety. Profile (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 119, family no. 15-3; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Samuel Tufts 2nd (41-2): born 19 Aug. 1752 in Medford; died 29 Nov. 1815; Lexington Alarm; Canada 1776; West Point 1780. Profile (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 160, family no. 41-2; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Samuel Tufts 3rd (44-3): born 16 Apr. 1761 in Medford; died 29 Aug. 1803; private at West Point and in Canada. Profile (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 166, family no. 44-3; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Conflict: death day of the Samuel born 1752
SourceSays
Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 52“died Nov. 20, 1815” (Samuel 3d)
Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 160died 29 Nov. 1815 (Samuel 2nd, 41-2)

Wild’s Samuel b. Aug. 16, 1732 (son of Joseph and Lydia, married Hannah Tufts, died 1818) is a clean match to 15-3. Wild’s Samuel b. April 1761 (son of Samuel and Hannah) is a clean match to 44-3. Wild’s Samuel 3d b. 1752 is a possible match to 41-2: the year agrees, but the death day differs and Vol. I numbering (“2nd”) differs from Wild’s (“3d”). The individual MSS entries on this page are still not assigned to any one man.

⚑ Unverified

Which Medford Samuel is which across sources. Wild p. 53 says Samuel 3d was “Called Jr., 1776,” so the MSS “Samuel, Jr.” and “Samuel (also given Samuel, Jr.)” entries could belong to different men. They are not merged. The Tufts Kinsmen Vol. I statements on this page come from an extract supplied by the owner and have not yet been checked against a page scan (citation rule: page scan not on file).
