Biography

William Tufts went to work at nine and rose from apprentice to one of Boston’s prosperous confectioners and caterers.

“He attended the public schools, but when he was nine years old was set to work to earn his own living. He was apprenticed to George Vinton, a prominent confectioner of Boston, and learned the business thoroughly.”

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

“He then embarked in the confectionery business on his own account, on Washington street, Boston, and to his confectionery trade added catering.”

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive) (continued on p. 1200) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Before going into business for himself he was foreman for Charles C. Copeland, a leading confectionery manufacturer. In 1876 he settled on Dwight Street in Boston’s South End, where he lived until his death on April 3, 1893. He was a Freemason of Lebanon Lodge and an Odd Fellow of Salome Lodge. Cutter adds that he later lost his sight but stayed active in business (Cutter, p. 1200).

“His religion was to “do unto others as you would have them do unto you.””

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1200. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Family Connection

Father: Uriah Tufts (1796–1860). Mother: Deborah Weston Thomas. Grandfather: Uriah Tufts (1768–1837).

Children by Mary Curtis Anthony: May Weston (b. 1843?); George Curtis (died in infancy, 1847); Esther O. (b. 1850). By Eunice Weston: Maria Faxon (b. 1857; m. Oscar G. Rankin); Jeannette Webster (m. John L. Flanigan); and William Phillips (died 1866). Maria’s son William Tufts Rankin (b. 1883) was adopted by his grandfather and renamed William Tufts Jr. (Cutter, p. 1200).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Uriah Tufts (1796–1860); grandfather Uriah Tufts (1768–1837).

Spouse(s)

  • Mary Curtis Anthony of Marblehead. Married November 1841. Died December 14, 1855.
  • Eunice Weston, born at Warren, Maine, in 1824, daughter of Edward and Margaret (Harriman) Weston. Married in Boston, January 1857. Died at her home in Boston, August 3, 1907 (Cutter, p. 1200).
