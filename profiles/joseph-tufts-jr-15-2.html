Biography

Capt. Joseph Tufts Jr. of Medford served the Revolution at home, on the town’s Committee of Correspondence and Safety. He died in December 1778.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: 1730/1 – 1778

Born 21 Feb. 1730/1 in Medford; died 6 Dec. 1778; married Hannah Cutter. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 119, family no. 15-2; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Served on the Committee of Correspondence and Safety; also served on the Grand Jury. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 119, family no. 15-2; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Family Connection
