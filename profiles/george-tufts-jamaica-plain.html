Biography

An eBay listing of a Jamaica Plain Gas Light Company bill, dated 1 July 1901, is titled as a bill of George Tufts. The photograph held here is a small crop of the bill’s heading, and the name is not readable on it.

George F. Tufts (1825–1920) of Charlestown and Medford was then living, but a Jamaica Plain gas account has not been shown to be his, and he is not identified with this bill.
