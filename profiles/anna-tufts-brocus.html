Biography

Anna Tufts was born in Medford on 25 February 1676, a daughter of Capt. Peter Tufts by his first wife, Elizabeth Lynde. The town record of her birth, printed as the earliest now extant in the Medford vital records, reads “Anna, d. Goodman, Feb. 25, 1676” (Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 146. Page (Internet Archive)). Wyman and the 1897 Tufts genealogy identify this Anna as Capt. Peter’s daughter (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 958. Page (Internet Archive); Larkin T. Tufts and Edward C. Booth, “Tufts Genealogy—Earlier Generations,” NEHGR 51 (July 1897), p. 300. Google Books).

On 24 June 1700 she married John Brocus at Medford. The 1897 genealogy names two children, Richard, born 20 November 1702, and Ann, born 23 March 1703/4 (Larkin T. Tufts and Edward C. Booth, “Tufts Genealogy—Earlier Generations,” NEHGR 51 (July 1897), p. 300. Google Books).

Marriage to John Brocus, 24 June 1700

The early Medford town records, as reprinted in the Medford Historical Register in 1931, give the marriage in its original spelling:

“the 24th day of June 1700. John Brocus & Anna Tufts was maried.”

Wilson Fiske and Ruth Dame Coolidge, “The Old Time Medford Town Meeting,” Medford Historical Register XXXIV, no. 4 (Dec. 1931), p. 78, reprinting the early Medford town records. View the page scan

The 1907 printed vital records of Medford enter the same marriage under both names, still as Brocus, at pp. 192 and 307 (Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 192. Page (Internet Archive); Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 307. Page (Internet Archive)). Wyman, writing in 1879, gives the husband as John Brookhouse (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 958. Page (Internet Archive)); the 1897 Tufts genealogy gives John Brocas (Brookhouse) (Larkin T. Tufts and Edward C. Booth, “Tufts Genealogy—Earlier Generations,” NEHGR 51 (July 1897), p. 300. Google Books).

A 1931 play, “The Old Time Medford Town Meeting,” brings “Mistress Peter Tufts and her daughter, Anna” to a neighbor’s fireside, Anna with John Brocus, “with whom her banns were cried last Sunday.” The authors treat Mercy Cotton, Capt. Peter Tufts’s second wife, as Anna’s stepmother. The dialogue is a dramatization; the authors’ notes and the reprinted town record are the historical sources. The scene is quoted, with the same clipping, on Capt. Peter Tufts’s profile.

Family Connection

Father: Capt. Peter Tufts (c. 1648–1721). Mother: Elizabeth Lynde (d. 1684), daughter of Ensign Thomas and Elizabeth Lynde of Malden. Stepmother: Mercy Cotton. Grandfather: Peter Tufts the immigrant. Half-brother and half-sister (children of Mercy Cotton): Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747); Sarah Tufts Hinckley (1702–1784). Children: Richard (b. 20 November 1702) and Ann (b. 23 March 1703/4) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 958. Page (Internet Archive); Larkin T. Tufts and Edward C. Booth, “Tufts Genealogy—Earlier Generations,” NEHGR 51 (July 1897), p. 300. Google Books).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Capt. Peter Tufts (c. 1648–1721); grandfather Peter Tufts the immigrant; half-brother Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747); half-sister Sarah Tufts Hinckley (1702–1784).
