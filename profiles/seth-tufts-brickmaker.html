Biography

After the brothers Isaac, Jonathan and Ebenezer Tufts had opened brick-yards on the south side of the Mystic, Brooks wrote, the work passed to a later pair:

“After these gentlemen came Seth Tufts, who, with his son Seth, carried on the business till recently. These yards were situated near Middlesex Canal and the river, about south-south-east from Rock Hill.”

Charles Brooks, History of the Town of Medford (1855), p. 356. Text (Perseus)

Brooks was writing in 1855, so the two Seths were still remembered as recent occupants of the yards then. The street location, between Summer and George Streets on Brickyard Lane, is given on the Businesses page. A Seth Tufts also appears among the Medford fishermen named in the town records (Brooks, p. 385).

⚑ Unverified

Brooks gives neither dates nor parentage for this Seth, or for his son. Wyman records a Seth, born 1774, son of Isaac Tufts and Martha of Medford (p. 961; Brooks, Register of Families, has the same son born 14 September 1774). That Seth is of the right generation to have run a yard “till recently” before 1855, but Brooks does not name the brickmaker as Isaac’s son, and the identification is not made here.
