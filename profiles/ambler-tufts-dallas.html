Biography

Ambler Tufts was the Dallas agent of the Consolidated Steel & Wire Company in 1897, and of the American Steel & Wire Company, under that later name, in 1902. His signature appears on the office letters of both years.

No birth, marriage, or death line, and no parents, were found for him in this pass. The name Ambler does not appear as a given name in the family record searched here.
