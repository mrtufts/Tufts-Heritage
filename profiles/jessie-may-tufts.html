Biography

Jessie May Tufts was a daughter of Albert C. Tufts. Sacramento city directories list her with her sister Edith at 1605 H Street in 1897, and in 1899 as “Tufts Jessie Miss, emp State Print[ing Office],” after Albert himself has dropped from the directory. She is named with Albert, Edith, and Mrs. Eva Nelson Tufts in the historic-churches index of St. Paul’s Episcopal Church, Sacramento (1852–1910). The directory record is gathered on her father’s profile.
