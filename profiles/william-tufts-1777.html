Biography

Among the many Medford Williams, one did not survive the war. Helen Tilden Wild lists him with the town’s soldiers:

“TUFTS, WILLIAM. Served at lines at Boston, 1776; in Col. Wesson's Regt., 1777. Son of William and Catherine (Tufts); born 1762; killed 1777.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 53 · page scan

His father was William Tufts Jr. (1727–1771), who as a youth of eighteen had been first into the Island Battery at Louisbourg in 1745 and who loaned money for Medford’s bounties in July 1776 (Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 62–63. Internet Archive). The family list of Revolutionary service remembers the son as a soldier killed at Bennington in 1777.

His record is among the entries on William Tufts (several men), which keeps the Massachusetts entries for every William separate.

The family record gives his birth as August 24, 1762, in Medford, and says he was a private killed at the Battle of Bennington in August 1777. His nephew Nathaniel Trask applied for a pension on behalf of his heirs in 1829 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165, no. 87-5). His father, the Louisbourg volunteer of 1745, died in 1771; his mother Katherine then married Nathaniel Wyman in 1772 and Ebenezer Richardson in 1776 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 81, no. 37-1).

The town history of Bedford lists a William Tufts among the “Men from Bedford who served for three years or the war. Called in 1777” (Abram English Brown, History of the Town of Bedford (1891), p. 67. Internet Archive), and the family record itself cites that page for him.

Service Record

Town: Medford, Mass. · Dates: 24 August 1762 – 1777

Wild (p. 53): service at the lines at Boston in 1776 and in Col. Wesson’s regiment in 1777; killed 1777. Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors (vol. XVI, p. 139) has a William Tufts of Bedford mustered in Capt. Pettengill’s company, Col. Wesson’s regiment, April 13, 1777; whether that is this man is not established.

Family Connection

Father: William Tufts Jr. (1727–1771), son of John and Sarah Tufts; mother: Catherine Tufts, who married second a Wyman and third a Richardson (Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 62–63. Internet Archive). Neither parent has a page here yet. Single so far as known.

Relatives with profiles on this site: William Tufts (several men): all Massachusetts entries for the name; uncle Ichabod Tufts (1731–1775?).

Spouse(s)

None recorded. He died young, in 1777.

⚑ Unverified

Day and place of death: the family record gives August 19, 1777, at Bennington; Wild says only “killed 1777,” and no muster or casualty roll recording his death has yet been seen. Bedford: that the Bedford three-year man of 1777 and the Bedford entry in Col. Wesson’s regiment are this Medford-born William is supported by the family record but not by an enlistment paper. Earlier birth year: a family list on another site gives about 1755; Wild and the family record both give 1762, which is followed here. Note: Pending further work.
