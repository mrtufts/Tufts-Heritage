Biography

George Tufts enlisted for three years. When he went home sick in 1780, the army recorded him as a deserter.

Service Record

Town: Medford; Concord · Dates: 1746/7 – 1796

Born 10 Jan. 1746/7 in Medford; died 25 Aug. 1796 in Concord. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 159, family no. 40-7)

Enlisted in the army for three years but was reported deserted in 1780 when he returned home sick. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 159, family no. 40-7)
