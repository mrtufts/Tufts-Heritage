Biography

Cotton Tufts, only child of Dr. Cotton Tufts and Lucy Quincy, was born in Weymouth:

“Cotton, s. Dr. Cotton and Lucy, Aug. 4, 1757.”

Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. I, Births (Boston: NEHGS, 1910). Book scan, births (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

The Adams Papers editors and the Harvard Quinquennial Catalogue place him in the Harvard class of 1777. Letters from his father to “Cotton Tufts Jr. student at Harvard” from 1774 to 1777 survive in the Weymouth Tufts family papers (Weymouth Public Libraries, Tufts / Smith / Adams Family Collection finding aid (Collection ID WCT002; prepared C. G. Swank, June 2016). Finding aid (PDF)).

Merchant of Tufts Corner

“His son Cotton Tufts (1757–1833) had a business/store with James Whittemore where the original Tufts Library building was built.”

Weymouth Public Libraries, Tufts / Smith / Adams Family Collection finding aid (Collection ID WCT002; prepared C. G. Swank, June 2016). Finding aid (PDF) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

“Now we come to the store occupied for years by Tufts and Whittemore. Here they kept a general store with the post office and our first circulating library, — the building then occupying the site of Tufts Library building in 1906.”

Edmund Soper Hunt, Weymouth Ways and Weymouth People: Reminiscences (Boston, 1907). Book scan, p. 23 (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Historic New England holds the firm’s 1823 stock book and other account books (1818–1833), listing yard goods, notions, hardware, tools and books (Historic New England, Tufts & Whittemore stock book, 1823 (collections access GUSN 194087). Catalog record). See Places: Tufts Corner.

Marriage and children

“Cotton Jr. and Mercy Brooks of Medford, int. Feb. 2, 1788.”

Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. II, Marriages and Deaths (Boston: NEHGS, 1910). Book scan, marriages (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

“Mercy and Cotton Tufts Jr. of Weymouth, Mar. 6, 1788.”

Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston: NEHGS, 1907). Book scan, marriages (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

His wife Mercy Brooks was his first cousin once the Medford and Weymouth lines are traced through the Brooks and Tufts families; the finding aid simply records the marriage and their four surviving children:

“They had four children: Quincy (1791–1872), Lucy (1793–1856) who married Thomas Tarbell, Susannah (1797–1877), and Mercy (1800–1858).”

Weymouth Public Libraries, Tufts / Smith / Adams Family Collection finding aid (Collection ID WCT002; prepared C. G. Swank, June 2016). Finding aid (PDF) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

“Quincy, s. Cotton Jr. [dup. omits Jr.] [Jr., c.r.i.] and Mercy, July 4, 1791. … Lucy, d. Cotton and Mercy, Sept. 13, 1793. … Susanna [dup. Susannah], d. Cotton and Mercy, Jan. 19, 1797. … Mercy, d. Cotton and Mercy, Nov. 19, 1800.”

Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. I, Births (Boston: NEHGS, 1910). Book scan, births (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Death

“Cotton Esq., Mar. 4, 1833, a. 76. [[h. Mercy (Brooks)] G.R.14.]”

Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. II, Marriages and Deaths (Boston: NEHGS, 1910). Book scan, deaths (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

G.R.14 is the North Weymouth Cemetery. His homestead at 246 Commercial Street is listed by the town as the Cotton Tufts Jr. Homestead (see Places).

Service Record

Town: Weymouth, Mass. · Dates: 1757–1833

Civilian merchant and postmaster at Weymouth Landing (Hunt, p. 23; finding aid). Harvard class of 1777 (Quinquennial Catalogue; Adams Papers editors). No entry under his name was checked in detail in Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War, vol. XVI (1907) for this pass.

Family Connection

Father: Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815). Mother: Lucy Quincy (d. 1785). Wife: Mercy Brooks (1763–1849). Children: Quincy; Lucy (m. Thomas Tarbell, May 5, 1813, Weymouth VR vol. II); Susannah; Mercy (1800–1858). Grandfather: Dr. Simon Tufts.

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Dr. Cotton Tufts; wife Mercy Brooks; son Quincy Tufts; daughter Susannah Tufts; grandfather Dr. Simon Tufts; stepmother Susanna Warner Tufts.

Spouse(s)
