Biography

Edmund Tufts was born on 13 August 1795, a son of Joseph Tufts and his first wife, Abigail Tufts, and died on 28 March 1859, aged 59 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 966. Page (Internet Archive)). He did not marry (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 966. Page (Internet Archive)).

Booth, writing of the Winter Hill homestead, remembered him as the printer of early Somerville:

“Edmund was intimately connected with the early history of this town, and his sign on the old house, ‘Edmund Tufts, Printer,’ is still remembered. For some years he did the printing for the new town of Somerville and its inhabitants, and we find his name on most of the early town reports. He issued a Somerville Directory in 1851, a pamphlet of thirty-two pages.”

Edward C. Booth, “The Tufts Family in Somerville,” Historic Leaves (Somerville Historical Society), vol. 1 (1902–03), p. 24. Text (Perseus)

The press is also on the Businesses and Places pages. Somerville was set off in 1842, in his lifetime.

Family Connection

Father: Joseph Tufts (1760–1818), deacon, son of Peter Tufts of Winter Hill and Anne Adams Tufts. Mother: Abigail Tufts (1761–1801). He was the eighth of eleven children of the first marriage, all living at his mother’s death in 1801 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 966. Page (Internet Archive)).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Joseph Tufts (1760–1818); mother Abigail Tufts (1761–1801); grandmother Anne Adams Tufts.
