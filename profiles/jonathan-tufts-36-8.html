Biography

Jonathan Tufts, brickmaker of Medford, served as a private in the lines around Boston.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: 1739 – 1784

Born 25 Feb. 1739 in Medford; died 26 Sep. 1784; brickmaker; private, served in the lines around Boston. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 153, family no. 36-8; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Family Connection
