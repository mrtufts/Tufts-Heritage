Biography

Daniel Tufts Jr. was the eldest son of Daniel Tufts, the Charlestown brick-maker and Abigail Tufts. Wyman records his birth as December 31, 1776, his marriage to Martha Bradshaw on December 26, 1809, and his death on September 21, 1868, in his ninety-second year (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. 2 (Boston, 1879), p. 968. Internet Archive).

“Daniel, junior, and Gilbert were tanners. Their tanneries and residences were within the peninsula. Daniel was a member of the Legislature from Charlestown in 1830, ’32, and ’33. He did considerable business for many years, but in advanced life he became very timid or over-cautious and so fell behind the times.”

Timothy T. Sawyer, Old Charlestown: Historical, Biographical, Reminiscent (Boston, 1902), p. 163. Internet Archive

“He had a stock of sole-leather in the vats of his tannery on Main Street which remained there unsold for years. When a purchaser was ready to meet the price he had been asking, it was always time to advance, and he didn’t live long enough to receive an offer he could accept. A kiln of bricks made for and belonging to him remained unsold until, in the accumulation of dust upon it, a willow-tree took root and grew up to its full size, an object for curious observation and remark.”

Timothy T. Sawyer, Old Charlestown: Historical, Biographical, Reminiscent (Boston, 1902), p. 163. Internet Archive

Wyman names four children: Charles, who died October 16, 1815, aged seven months and seven days; Daniel, who died October 20, 1815, aged nine days, with a gravestone in the “new ground”; Martha, who married Thatcher Magoun of Medford; and Abby, who married James C. Neilson, M.D., and who died October 5, 1871, aged fifty-one years, one month and fifteen days, with the funeral from 193 Main Street (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. 2 (Boston, 1879), p. 968. Internet Archive). Sawyer gives his age at death as ninety-two (pp. 163–164).

Service Record

Town: Charlestown, Mass. · Dates: 31 December 1776–21 September 1868

Born in 1776, he was too young for Revolutionary service. He represented Charlestown in the Massachusetts House in 1830, 1832 and 1833 (Sawyer, p. 163).

Family Connection

Father: Daniel Tufts (1753–1839), brick-maker. Mother: Abigail Tufts (Wyman, p. 968). Brothers: Gilbert Tufts (1778–1850), Charles Tufts (1781–1876) and Nathan Tufts “2d” (1786–1872). Grandfather: Nathan Tufts, cordwainer. Uncles: Deacon Amos Tufts and Nathan Tufts of Charlestown. Wife: Martha Bradshaw, married December 26, 1809. Children as named by Wyman, p. 968: Charles (d. 1815), Daniel (d. 1815), Martha (m. Thatcher Magoun of Medford), and Abby (m. James C. Neilson, M.D.; d. 1871).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Daniel Tufts, brick-maker; brother Gilbert Tufts (1778–1850); brother Charles Tufts (1781–1876); brother Nathan Tufts “2d” (1786–1872); grandfather Nathan Tufts, cordwainer; uncle Deacon Amos Tufts; uncle Nathan Tufts of Charlestown.

Spouse(s)

  • Martha (Bradshaw) Tufts. Married December 26, 1809 (Wyman, vol. 2, p. 968). Her dates and parentage are not given there.
