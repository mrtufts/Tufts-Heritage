Eliakim Tufts was born in Medford on September 4, 1767, a son of William and Katherine (Tufts) Tufts. He married Sarah Ross at Billerica on May 8, 1788; their children were born at Groton, and the family later lived at Littleton, where several of the children died within a few years. After Sarah’s death he entered intentions of marriage at Littleton in 1823, and he died on February 19, 1825.

The family record notes his name on a list of Billerica men who served three years in the Continental Army; the original roll has not been seen here.

Biography

Eliakim Tufts was born in Medford on September 4, 1767, a son of William and Katherine (Tufts) Tufts. He married Sarah Ross at Billerica on May 8, 1788 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165, no. 87-6). Their children were born at Groton from the 1790s to about 1804 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 73–75), and the family later lived at Littleton.

The Family at Littleton

Within four years the Littleton town clerk entered four deaths in the family on a single page:

“Eliakim Ross Tufts. Son of Eliakim Tufts. & Sarah his wife Died April 17. 1821. Sarah Tufts, wife of Eliakim Tufts. Died July 1st 1822. Ebenezer Tufts, Son of Said Eliakim Died December 31th 1823 The above said Eliakim Tufts. Died February 19th 1825.”

Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 205. Internet Archive

Between those losses he remarried. The town’s intentions of marriage record “Mr Eliakim Tufts of Littleton, and Mrs Merriam Raymond of Amhurst State of Newhampshire, July 6 1823” (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 281. Internet Archive). She was Miriam (Leonard) Raymond, widow of the Hon. David Raymond; the family record has the marriage at New Durham, New Hampshire, in July 1823, and her death at Amherst, New Hampshire, on December 29, 1838 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165). A few weeks after his death, the intention of “Leonard Porter of Carlisle and Daranda Tufts of Littleton, May 1st 1825” was entered (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 282. Internet Archive). She was his daughter Dorinda.

In the DAR Records

The DAR lists him as DAR Ancestor no. A116790: private, Massachusetts, “from Billerica”; born September 14, 1767, at Medford.

See also his brother Ebenezer Tufts (1761–1830).

Service Record

Town: Medford; Billerica; Groton; Littleton, Mass. · Dates: 4 September 1767 – 19 February 1825

The family record notes that his name appears on a list of Billerica soldiers who served three years in the Continental Army during the Revolution (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165, no. 87-6, citing a D.A.R. list). No muster roll naming him has yet been seen here.

Family Connection

Parents: William Tufts and Katherine (Tufts) Tufts (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 163). Children (thirteen are listed): Sarah, Catherine, William, Catherine, Eliakim (bapt. 1797, died young), Mary (b. 1798, m. Benjamin Brown), Lucy (b. 1800, m. Brooks Bowman), Eliakim Ross (1801–1821), the twins Levi and Achsah (b. 1804), Dorinda, George, and Ebenezer (d. 1823) (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165; Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 74–75).

Relatives with profiles on this site: brother Ebenezer Tufts (1761–1830); mother Katherine (Tufts) Tufts (b. 1734); daughter Dorinda (“Daranda”) Tufts.

Spouse(s)

  • Sarah Ross (b. January 21, 1765, at Billerica; d. July 1, 1822, at Littleton), daughter of John and Mary (Dunckle) Ross; married May 8, 1788, at Billerica (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165; Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 205. Internet Archive).
  • Miriam (Leonard) Raymond (b. 1765; d. December 29, 1838, at Amherst, N.H.), widow of the Hon. David Raymond; intention recorded at Littleton, July 6, 1823 (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 281. Internet Archive; Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 165).
