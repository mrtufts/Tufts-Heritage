Biography

Nathaniel Tufts was born at Medford on 23 February 1692, a son of John Tufts of Malden (himself a son of Peter Tufts the immigrant) and Mary (Putnam) Tufts, and a brother of Peter Tufts (1697–1776) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)).

He married, first, Mary Sprague on 7 April 1715; she died on 15 December 1715, in her 26th year, and was buried at Charlestown. He married, second, Mary Rand on 18 October 1716; she died on 26 December 1764, aged 70, and was buried at Cambridge. Nathaniel died on 27 August 1741, “in 53d yr.,” with a gravestone (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)). His children by the second marriage, as Wyman gives them, were Nathaniel (baptized 4 August 1717); William (died 1719, an infant) and William (born 30 June 1721); Mary (born 11 January 1722/3, m. John Morse); David (born 25 February 1724); John (born 13 February 1727); Timothy (born 2 February 1728/9); Persis (born 27 May 1733); and Isaiah (baptized 20 July 1740) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)).

Booth places his homestead on Washington Street near Union Square. By the Revolution the lot had passed to his son William, who died in 1773. A barrack was erected on the homestead lot during the siege of Boston, and Fort No. 3 took its beginning near the same point (Edward C. Booth, “The Tufts Family in Somerville,” Historic Leaves (Somerville Historical Society), vol. 1 (1902–03), part 1, pp. 22–23. Text (Perseus)). The homestead is on the Places page. Wyman’s William 33, brother of Nathaniel’s eldest surviving son, died on 2 November 1773, aged 53, which fits Booth’s William who died in 1773 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 964. Page (Internet Archive)).

Family Connection

Father: John Tufts of Malden (d. 28 March 1728, aged 63), son of Peter Tufts the immigrant. Mother: Mary Putnam. Brother: Peter Tufts (1697–1776), father of Peter Tufts of Winter Hill. Uncle: Jonathan Tufts (c. 1659–1722) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)).

Relatives with profiles on this site: uncle Jonathan Tufts (c. 1659–1722); grandfather Peter Tufts the immigrant; brother Peter Tufts (1697–1776); nephew Peter Tufts of Winter Hill.

Spouse(s)

  • Mary Sprague (d. 15 December 1715, in her 26th year, gravestone at Charlestown). Married 7 April 1715 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)).
  • Mary Rand (d. 26 December 1764, aged 70, gravestone at Cambridge). Married 18 October 1716. Administration on her estate was granted to her son William on 22 January 1765 (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 963. Page (Internet Archive)).
