Biography

John M. Tufts manufactured office furniture and “the celebrated Tufts Roll-Top Desks” from 131 Fulton Street, at the northeast corner of Nassau Street, in New York, with a factory at Somerville, Massachusetts. A letter of 12 February 1892, signed in his name, discusses a shipment of desks from the Boston factory and states that more than 7,000 of the desks were then in use in New York City.

The Somerville factory is a local clue, but no birth, marriage, or death line, and no parents, were found for him in this pass. Other men named John M. Tufts appear in the family record; none was shown to be this manufacturer.
