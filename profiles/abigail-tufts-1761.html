Biography

Abigail Tufts was born on 24 July 1761, a daughter of James Tufts and Tabitha (Binford) Tufts of Medford, and a sister of James Tufts, potter (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 960. Page (Internet Archive)).

On 31 March 1783 she married Joseph Tufts, son of Peter Tufts of Winter Hill and Anne Adams Tufts. She died at Medford, in child-bed, on 9 October 1801, aged 40 (39 on her tablet), and was carried to Charlestown (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 966. Page (Internet Archive)). Joseph later married Hannah Williams, and died on 17 December 1818, aged 58.

All eleven of their children were by Abigail, and all were living at her death: Joseph (b. 5 October 1783); Abigail (b. 24 March 1785, still single at Somerville in 1866); Lydia (1786–1808); Bernard (b. 27 August 1788); Asa (b. 25 May 1790); Lucy (b. 6 January 1792); Mary (1793–1820); Edmund, the printer (1795–1859); Mercy (1797–1820); Harriet (b. 7 April 1799); and Caroline (b. 17 September 1801) (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 966. Page (Internet Archive)).
