Biography

A bill form of the 1890s is headed “To TUFTS & NEAL, Dr., Main St.,” and is dated at Rangeley, Maine. The first name of the Tufts partner is not given, and no other paper of the firm has been seen here.

A Baker Tufts, boat builder, born 26 May 1823 at Farmington, Maine, died at Rangeley on 27 July 1898 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 282, no. 155-6). He lived at Rangeley in the right decade, but it has not been shown that he was the Tufts of this firm.
