Biography

Ebenezer Tufts, farmer, was born in Medford on April 19, 1761, a son of William and Katherine (Tufts) Tufts and an older brother of Eliakim Tufts. He married Hannah Livingston at Billerica in 1781, and the family later moved to Cheshire County, New Hampshire. Hannah died at Marlborough in 1802, and he died at Roxbury, New Hampshire, on November 27, 1830 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 164, no. 87-4).

The DAR lists him as DAR Ancestor no. A116789: private, Massachusetts, in Capt. John Wood’s company, Col. Loammi Baldwin’s regiment, with a Revolutionary pension (file “S”); born April 19, 1761, at Medford; died November 27, 1830, at Roxbury, N.H.

Family Connection

Parents: William and Katherine (Tufts) Tufts. Brother: Eliakim Tufts (1767–1825). Children by Hannah: Hannah, Catherine, William, Ebenezer, Betsey, Eliakim, Lydia, Catherine, and Phebe. By Elizabeth: Abigail and Eliakim (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 164).

Relatives with profiles on this site: brother Eliakim Tufts (1767–1825).

Spouse(s)

  • Hannah Livingston (1760–1802), daughter of Thomas and Phebe (Stone) Livingston of Billerica; married April 27, 1781, at Billerica.
  • Elizabeth (Betsey) Travis (b. 1765, Holliston), daughter of John and Anna (Maxwell) Travis; married November 25, 1802, in Sullivan County, N.H.; living in 1820 at Detroit with her stepdaughter Phebe (Tufts) Scales (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 164).
