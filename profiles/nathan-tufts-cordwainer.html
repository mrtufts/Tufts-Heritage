Biography

Nathan Tufts was a shoemaker by trade who made his living mostly by farming in Medford. He was a son of Peter Tufts of the Milk Row road in Charlestown (now Somerville), and his three sons became leading business men of Charlestown: a brick-maker, a blacksmith, and a butcher, tanner and mill owner.

“One of the sons of this Peter was Nathan Tufts, by trade a cordwainer, but chiefly occupied as a farmer in Medford. He was the father of Daniel, Deacon Amos, and Nathan Tufts, who for the greater part of their lives were prominent citizens of the old town and among its leading business men.”

Timothy T. Sawyer, Old Charlestown: Historical, Biographical, Reminiscent (Boston, 1902), p. 162. Internet Archive

His sons on this site are Deacon Amos Tufts (c. 1762–1839) and Nathan Tufts of Charlestown (c. 1764–1835). Sawyer describes the eldest, Daniel, as a brick-maker and farmer who lived outside Charlestown Neck and sat in the Legislature in 1811 and 1812 (p. 162).
