Biography

Grimes Tufts, a Medford brickmaker, turned out as a minuteman on the Lexington Alarm and rose to lieutenant.

Service Record

Town: Medford; Lynn · Dates: 1748 – 1808

Born 4 Dec. 1748 in Medford; died 23 Dec. 1808 in Lynn; brickmaker. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 159, family no. 40-8; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Minuteman who answered the Lexington Alarm; Lieutenant during the Revolution. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 159, family no. 40-8; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)

Family Connection

Family number 40-8 in Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I. Parents are not named in the extract seen.
