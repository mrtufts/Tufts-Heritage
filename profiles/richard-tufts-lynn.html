Biography

The 1890 History of Lynn gives a short sketch of Deacon Richard Tufts. He was born in Lynn, the son of a Revolutionary soldier:

“Deacon Tufts was born in Lynn, and was a son of David Tufts, a corporal in the army of the Revolution, who, after the war was ended, took up the peaceful and multifarious employments of farmer, trader, and common carrier, all in a limited way. He owned and occupied a house that stood on the south-east corner of Federal street and Western avenue.”

History of Lynn, Essex County, Massachusetts (1890), pp. 212–213. Internet Archive (Transcribed from the printed page.)

As a young man the deacon became known for his zeal in the temperance cause, and for many years he held the office of deacon in the First Church of Lynn, holding firmly to its Calvinist faith. The sketch closes with his son:

“His son, Gardiner Tufts, was prominent in the civil war, for his efficient services in Washington and elsewhere in behalf of the Massachusetts soldiery. And since the close of the war he has acceptably filled several important public positions”

History of Lynn, Essex County, Massachusetts (1890), pp. 212–213. Internet Archive
