Biography

Aaron Tufts was a son of Peter Tufts Jr. and Hannah Adams. The family record gives his birth as 5 November 1793, at Medford, and his death as 8 May 1881, at Pavilion, Genesee County, New York, and calls him a farmer (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 235, no. 129-10; Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 145. Page (Internet Archive)). Booth says that Thomas and Aaron settled in New York State (Edward C. Booth, “The Tufts Family in Somerville,” Historic Leaves (Somerville Historical Society), vol. 1 (1902–03), p. 22. Text (Perseus)). Wyman names Aaron among the children of Peter 38, without dates (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 965. Page (Internet Archive)). He is not the earlier Aaron Tufts (1761–1781), who died a prisoner at Mill Prison.

He married, first, on 21 September 1817 at Bethany, New York, Mehitable Pearson, who died at Pavilion on 7 April 1831; they had six children. He married, second, in March 1833 at Covington, New York, Keziah (Townsend) Perry, a widow; they had no children (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 235, no. 129-10).

Service Record

Town: Medford, Mass.; later Pavilion, Genesee County, N.Y. · Dates: 5 November 1793 – 8 May 1881

The family record states that he served New York as a soldier in the War of 1812 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 236). No muster roll has been seen in this pass.

Family Connection

Father: Peter Tufts Jr. (1753–1832). Mother: Hannah Adams (d. 1816). Brothers and sisters with profiles on this site: Peter Tufts the surveyor, Joel, Thomas, Sally (Tufts) Locke.

Relatives with profiles on this site: sister Sally (Tufts) Locke (1789–1871); brother Joel Tufts (1781–1852); brother Thomas Tufts (1785–1844); father Peter Tufts Jr. (1753–1832); brother Peter Tufts the surveyor (1774–1825); Aaron Tufts (1761–1781) of Medford, a different man.

Spouse(s)

  • Mehitable Pearson (19 December 1794 – 7 April 1831), daughter of William Ely and Mehitable (Reeves) Pearson. Married 21 September 1817, at Bethany, N.Y. Six children (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 235).
  • Keziah (Townsend) Perry (1799 – 18 October 1876), widow. Married March 1833, at Covington, N.Y. No children (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 235).
