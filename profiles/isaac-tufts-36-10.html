Biography

Isaac Tufts answered the Lexington alarm and served as a sergeant, including at Dorchester Heights.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: 1744 – 1823

Born 10 Oct. 1744 in Medford; died 22 July 1823; sergeant; answered the Lexington alarm; served at Dorchester Heights. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 154, family no. 36-10)
