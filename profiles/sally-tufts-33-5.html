Biography

Sally (Sarah) Tufts was born at Newburyport on January 30, 1772, the daughter of Samuel and Sarah: “Sally, d. Samuel and Sarah, Jan. 30, 1772.” Vital Records of Newburyport, Massachusetts, to the End of the Year 1849, vol. 1, Births (Salem, 1911), p. 399. The same page records an earlier sister Sarah, born September 21, 1763, who is not this woman. Her father was Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799); her mother was Sarah (Moody) Tufts.

On July 29, 1797, Elizabeth Smith Shaw Peabody wrote to Abigail Adams that Samuel Tufts’s “Son, & daughter now lie at the point of death.” Founders Online, Elizabeth Smith Shaw Peabody to Abigail Adams, 29 July 1797. The letter does not name them. The Adams Papers editorial note identifies the daughter as Sally and gives her death as 15 August 1797, citing the Boston Columbian Centinel of 19 August and the Newburyport Political Gazette of 10 and 17 August.

Vital Records of Newburyport, Massachusetts, to the End of the Year 1849, vol. 2, Marriages and Deaths (Salem, 1911), p. 821: “Sally, d. Samuel and Sarah, Aug. 15, 1797.” The line does not say that she was single. Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 73, no. 33-5, says she died unmarried; that last point is not stated on the tan-book death line. The August 1797 newspaper page images have not been obtained from free sources. A compiled American Antiquarian Society index of Columbian Centinel obituaries lists “Tufts, Sally, dau. of Samuel Tufts d. in Newburyport, aged 24 yrs. (C.C. Aug. 19, 1797)” (Index of Obituaries in Massachusetts Centinel and Columbian Centinel, 1784–1840, vol. 5, p. 4580). Age 24 in August 1797 is consistent with the 30 January 1772 birth.

Service Record

Town: Newburyport, Mass. · Dates: 30 Jan. 1772 – 15 Aug. 1797

No military service is recorded or expected for her. She is noticed here as a daughter of Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport.

Family Connection

Parents: Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799) and Sarah (Moody) Tufts. Brother treated on this site: Simon Tufts of Newburyport (1769–1797). Other children of Samuel and Sarah recorded at Newburyport (vol. 1, p. 399) include Sarah, Sept. 21, 1763; Samuel, Aug. 30, 1765; Dolly, Mar. 22, 1767; Simon, Mar. 7, 1769; and Polly, Feb. 4, 1775. Uncles: Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815) and Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786). Grandfather: Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747).

Relatives with profiles on this site: brother Simon Tufts of Newburyport (1769–1797); father Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799); uncle Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815); uncle Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786); grandfather Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747).

Spouse(s)

No marriage has been found in the sources seen. Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 73, no. 33-5, says she died single; that statement has not been confirmed from the Newburyport marriage or death volumes.
