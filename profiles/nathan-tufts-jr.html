Biography

Nathan Tufts Jr. helped pay for the men sent to New York in 1776 and served 82 days with Capt. Benjamin Blaney’s guards at Cambridge in 1778.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: [dates not confirmed]

“Tufts, Nathan, Jr. Account of money paid by persons to hire men who went to New York in Sept., 1776, examined and allowed by a committee at Medford Jan. 13, 1777; also, Private, Capt. Benjamin Blaney's co., Col. Eleazer Brooks's regt. of guards; joined Jan. 12, 1778; service to April 3, 1778, 82 days, at Cambridge.”

Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War, vol. XVI (1907), p. 137

From Cutter’s Memoirs (1910)

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive) (As read in the scanned text.)
