Biography

A Medford gravestone records the death of “Tufts, Nathan, Mr., Dec. 21, 1771. [a. 48, g.r.i.].” Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston, 1907), p. 444. Age 48 on that date implies a birth about 1723–24.

Charles Brooks, History of the Town of Medford (Boston, 1855), p. 545, register no. 38-65, places a Nathan, born May 14, 1724, among the children of Peter Tufts of Milk Row and Lydia Buckman. The same register, p. 546, says “Nathan Tufts m. Mary Adams, June 6, 1751. He lived at Charlestown; where he died, Dec. 21, 1771,” and lists children including Susanna, Mary, Abigail, Daniel, Amos, and Nathan, with an earlier son Nathan who died young (Brooks prints the child’s death as 1702, which is a typographical error for 1762; see Medford VR, p. 444, below). The printed death is in the Medford volume, with a Medford gravestone; Brooks’s “died at Charlestown” is not followed here.

The Cambridge (Menotomy) marriage is in the tan book: “Tufts, Nathan (of Charlestown, int.) and Mary Adams, June 6, 1751.” Vital Records of Cambridge, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. 2, Marriages and Deaths (Boston, 1915), p. 396. The same date is in Benjamin and William R. Cutter, History of the Town of Arlington, Massachusetts (Boston, 1880), p. 184, under Mary, daughter of Lieut. Joseph and Rachel Adams, “b. 12, bap. 20 May, 1733 … m. Nathan Tufts, of Charlestown, 6 June, 1751 (she m. second, Richard Clark of Watertown).” The widow’s second marriage is in Medford VR, p. 310: “Mary and Richard Clarke of Watertown, Nov. 25, 1777.* [Clark.”

Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. 2 (Boston, 1879), p. 967, Nathan 48, calls him son of Peter 36, a cordwainer, married to Mary Adams June 6, 1751, died December 21, 1771, and gives issue: Nathan, b. June 7, 1752, d. Aug. 5, 1762; Daniel, Dec. 27, 1753; Susanna, Jan. 31, 1756; Mary, March 17, 1758; Abigail, Jan. 20, 1760; Amos, July 30, 1762; Nathan, March 23, 1764. Administration to the widow, January 14, 1772; inventory total £267. Medford VR confirms several of those children: Susanna, Jan. 31, 1756 (p. 153); Mary, Mar. 17, 1758 (p. 150); Abigail, Jan. 20, 1760 (p. 146); Nathan, Mar. 23, 1764 (p. 151); and the death of Nathan, s. Nathan and Mary, Aug. 5, 1762, “in 11th y., g.r.i.” (p. 444).

Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 50, no. 18-1, is the same compiled man (“farmer, cordwainer”), born 14 May 1724 in Charlestown (now Somerville), citing Charlestown VR, vol. 1, p. 301. That printed line is confirmed from Roger D. Joslyn, comp., Vital Records of Charlestown, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. 1 (Boston, 1984), p. 301, under original record “[p. 34] Births in Charlestown”: “Nathan, son of Peter Tufts & Lydia his wife, b. May 14, 1724.” He died 21 Dec. 1771 at Medford (Medford VR, p. 444). Kinsmen prints the marriage as 6 June 1750, which conflicts with the Cambridge tan book, Brooks, Cutter, and Wyman, all of which have 6 June 1751. This page follows the vital record.

Identity caveat: this may be the same person as Nathan Tufts, cordwainer, of Medford, the farmer-cordwainer of Medford, son of Peter of Milk Row, already on this site. The cordwainer page, drawn from Sawyer, did not then have these dates, and flagged a possible confusion with a later Nathan. The dates, wife, children, and occupation gathered here fit that man, but the two pages have not been merged. WikiTree Tufts-408 is the compiled identity for Nathan Tufts (1724–1771).

Service Record

Town: Charlestown (now Somerville); Medford, Mass. · Dates: 14 May 1724 – 21 Dec. 1771

He died before the Lexington alarm. No Revolutionary service is recorded or expected.

Family Connection

Parents: Peter Tufts of Milk Row and Lydia (Bucknam) Tufts (Joslyn, Charlestown VR, vol. 1, p. 301; Brooks, p. 545, no. 4-38 / 38-65; Wyman, vol. 2, p. 967, Nathan 48, son of Peter 36). Wife: Mary Adams, married 6 June 1751 (Cambridge VR, vol. 2, p. 396); she married, second, Richard Clark of Watertown, 25 Nov. 1777 (Medford VR, p. 310). Children recorded at Medford: Susanna (p. 153), Mary (p. 150), Abigail (p. 146), Nathan b. 23 Mar. 1764 (p. 151), and Nathan who died 5 Aug. 1762 in his 11th year (p. 444). Wyman and Brooks also list Daniel (b. 27 Dec. 1753) and Amos (b. 30 July 1762). Sons with profiles on this site, if this is the cordwainer: Deacon Amos Tufts (1762–1839) and Nathan Tufts (c. 1764–1835).

Relatives with profiles on this site: possibly the same man as Nathan Tufts, cordwainer, of Medford (pages not merged).

Spouse(s)

  • Mary Adams, daughter of Lieut. Joseph and Rachel (Allen) Adams of Menotomy (Cutter, History of Arlington, p. 184; Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 50). Married June 6, 1751 (Cambridge VR, vol. 2, p. 396). She married, second, Richard Clark of Watertown, November 25, 1777 (Medford VR, p. 310). Cutter and Kinsmen give her birth as 12 May 1733 and baptism 20 May 1733.
