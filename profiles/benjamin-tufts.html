Biography

Benjamin Tufts helped pay for Medford’s soldiers sent to Canada and to New York in 1776, and served himself in Capt. Benjamin Blaney’s company of guards at Cambridge in 1778. A Benjamin Tufts was also one of the brickmakers of the Fountain Yards (see Businesses).

In the DAR Records

The family record and the DAR both describe a Benjamin Tufts of Medford, born November 15, 1731, and died there July 14, 1804, a son of Benjamin and Mary (Hutchinson) Tufts. He married first Esther Lynde, in 1760, and second Lydia Francis, in 1779 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 53, no. 19-1). The DAR lists him as DAR Ancestor no. A116786 (“Benjamin Tufts Sr.”), with patriotic service, “paid account.” That fits the 1776 payments toward Medford’s soldiers described above. The Sarah Bradlee Fulton Chapter marked a Revolutionary grave for a Benjamin Tufts in the Salem Street burying ground in May 1897 (DAR Genealogical Research System, Revolutionary War grave and marker records).
