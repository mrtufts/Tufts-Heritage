Biography

Sarah Tufts was a daughter of Capt. Peter Tufts of Medford by his second wife, Mercy Cotton. Through her mother she was a great-granddaughter of Gov. Simon Bradstreet and the poet Anne Bradstreet (Brooks, p. 544). She was still single when her father made his will in 1719, which named “daus. … Sarah Tufts” among his legatees and left her brothers land at Quaboag, the later Brookfield (Wyman, p. 958). She married a Brookfield man and spent the rest of her long life there.

“Sarah, b. 13 May, 1702; m. 9 Dec., 1723, Job, son of Samuel and Sarah (Pope) Hinckley, who was b. 16 Feb., 1687-8, at Brookfield, and there d. 4 July, 1753. She d. 4 Sept., 1784.”

Larkin T. Tufts and Edward C. Booth, “Tufts Genealogy—Earlier Generations,” New England Historical and Genealogical Register, vol. 51 (July 1897), p. 301. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Family Connection

Father: Capt. Peter Tufts. Mother: Mercy Cotton. Brother: Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747). Grandfather: Peter Tufts the immigrant. Wyman (p. 958) lists her as “xv. Sarah, m. Job Hinkley.”

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Capt. Peter Tufts; brother Dr. Simon Tufts; grandfather Peter Tufts the immigrant.

Spouse(s)

  • Job Hinckley (1688–1753), son of Samuel and Sarah (Pope) Hinckley, born at Brookfield February 16, 1687/8. Married December 9, 1723; died at Brookfield July 4, 1753 (NEHGR 51, p. 301).
