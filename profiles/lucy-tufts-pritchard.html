Biography

Lucy Tufts was the second child of William Tufts by his second wife, Mary Francis. She grew up in Medford among a large family of half-brothers and brothers, several of whom served in the Revolution, and married two months before the fighting began at Lexington and Concord.

“Lucy, d. William and Mary, Apr. 21, 1754.”

Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 150. Page (Internet Archive)

“Prichard (see Pritchard), Thomas and Lucy Tufts 2d, Feb. 22, 1775.”

Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 279. Page (Internet Archive) (“2d” distinguishes her from another Lucy Tufts of the town.)

“Lucy, born April 21, 1754; married Thomas Pritchard.”

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive)

Her husband is “Capt. Thom[a]s” in the record of their daughter Attalanta’s birth, June 4, 1787 (Medford VRs, p. 114). Attalanta married Zebina Bailey on November 13, 1816 (p. 279).

Family Connection

Father: Lieut. William Tufts. Mother: Mary Francis. Brothers and half-brothers: William, George, Grimes, Samuel, David, Richard and Moses. Brooks (p. 550) lists her as no. 262, “Lucy, b. Apr. 21, 1754; m. Thomas Pritchard.”

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Lieut. William Tufts; grandfather James Tufts (1672–1733); brother Richard Tufts; brother Samuel Tufts; brother David Tufts; half-brother William Tufts Jr..

Spouse(s)

  • Thomas Pritchard (also Prichard). Married at Medford, February 22, 1775 (Medford VRs, p. 279). Called “Capt. Thomas” in 1787 (p. 114).
