Biography

James Tufts, a younger son of Peter Tufts the immigrant, is the first of the family known to have died in war. In King Philip’s War he was a teamster with Capt. Thomas Lathrop’s company, which was ambushed while escorting carts of grain from Deerfield on September 18, 1675, at the stream afterward called Bloody Brook.

“James2 (Peter1), resident of Deerfield; was with Capt. Lothrop as teamster when the latter was ambuscaded at Bloody Brook, and was there killed, 18 September, 1675.”

Larkin T. Tufts and Edward C. Booth, “Tufts Genealogy—Earlier Generations,” New England Historical and Genealogical Register, vol. 51 (July 1897), p. 301. Page (Internet Archive)

“James, son of Peter Tufts, was born in Charlestown, about 1650. He was a soldier in King Philip's war, and was killed in the battle of Bloody Brook, September 18, 1675.”

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1198. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

“James 7. Son of Peter 1; soldier at Bloody Brook, and there was slain Sept. 18. 1675.”

Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 959. Page (Internet Archive)

He left one child, James Tufts (1672–1733), whom the immigrant remembered in his will with “new house near Mystic bridge; 40 acres near house” (Wyman, p. 958). Through that grandson the line runs to Lieut. William Tufts of Medford.

Service Record

Town: Charlestown and Deerfield, Mass. · Dates: c. 1650–1675

King Philip’s War: teamster with Capt. Lathrop’s company; killed at Bloody Brook (South Deerfield), September 18, 1675 (NEHGR 51, p. 301; Wyman, p. 959).

Family Connection

Father: Peter Tufts the immigrant. Brother: Capt. Peter Tufts. Son: James Tufts (1672–1733), by Elizabeth Wells (see Spouse).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Peter Tufts the immigrant; brother Capt. Peter Tufts; son James Tufts (1672–1733); grandson Lieut. William Tufts.

Spouse(s)

  • Elizabeth Wells. Cutter (p. 1198) says he “married Elizabeth Wells.” Wyman (p. 959) records only a court case with her about 1669 and a Charlestown selectmen’s order of June 27, 1672, for the “Child of James Tufts and Elizabeth Wells, to be kept by Susanna White.” No marriage record has been found.

⚑ Unverified

Birth: “about 1650” is Cutter’s estimate; no record found. Brooks’s confusion: Brooks (1855, p. 550) says a James “said (B) to have been a son of Peter (No. 1)” married Mary Dill in 1729 and was “supposed to have been killed by the Indians.” The Dill marriage belongs to his son (NEHGR, Wyman), so Brooks has run father and son together.
