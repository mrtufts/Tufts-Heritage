Biography

John Tufts was born in Medford on March 15, 1738/9, a son of Benjamin and Mary (Hutchinson) Tufts, and lived in Malden. In June 1776 he went with Capt. Benjamin Blaney’s company on the expedition to Point Shirley, now Winthrop. He was head of a household in the 1790 census and died at Malden on March 23, 1799 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 55, no. 19-5).

On Malden’s Revolutionary Tablet

A John Tufts is named on Malden’s bronze tablet to the soldiers and sailors who enlisted from the town, along with Adam, Stephen and Timothy Tufts. Capt. Benjamin Blaney heads its list.

Service Record

Town: Medford and Malden, Mass. · Dates: 15 March 1738/9 – 23 March 1799

The DAR lists him as DAR Ancestor no. A116803: private, Massachusetts, in Capt. Benjamin Blaney’s company; born March 15, 1739, at Medford; died February 23, 1799, at Malden. The family record adds the Point Shirley expedition of June 1776 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 55).

Family Connection

Parents: Benjamin and Mary (Hutchinson) Tufts. A brother, Benjamin, is probably the Benjamin Tufts of Medford on this site. Children by his first wife: John, Asa, and Josiah. By his second: Elias and Peter (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 55).

Relatives with profiles on this site: probable brother Benjamin Tufts; cousin Stephen Tufts Jr. (1749–1832); also on the Malden tablet: Adam Tufts.

Spouse(s)

  • Rebecca Hawkes (1746–1769), daughter of John and Hannah (Priest) Hawkes of Lynn; married April 28, 1763, at Lynn.
  • Lois Taylor (1748–1834), daughter of Ebenezer and Mary (Gowing) Taylor of Reading; married June 15, 1773, at Reading (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 55).
