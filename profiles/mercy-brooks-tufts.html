Biography

Mercy Brooks was born in Medford:

“Mercy, d. Thomas [Thomas Jr., c.r.i.] and Mercy Jr., Sept. 3, 1763.”

Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston: NEHGS, 1907). Book scan, births (under Brooks) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Her mother was Mercy (Tufts) Brooks; the Medford marriage record of Dec. 29, 1762, names Mercy Tufts and Thomas Brooks Jr. (Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston: NEHGS, 1907). Book scan, marriages). She married Cotton Tufts Jr. of Weymouth in 1788 (intentions at Weymouth, Feb. 2; marriage at Medford, Mar. 6).

The Weymouth collection holds her diary for the year 1799 (Weymouth Public Libraries, Tufts / Smith / Adams Family Collection finding aid (Collection ID WCT002; prepared C. G. Swank, June 2016). Finding aid (PDF), Series II). She raised four children who lived to adulthood, including the library founders Quincy and Susannah.

“Mercy, b. Medford, wid. Cotton, d. Tho[ma]s Brooks and Mercy, old age, May 6, 1849, a. 85 y. 7 m. 4 d. [a. 86, G.R.14.]”

Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. II, Marriages and Deaths (Boston: NEHGS, 1910). Book scan, deaths (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)
