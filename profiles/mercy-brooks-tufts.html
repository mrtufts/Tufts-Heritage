Biography

Mercy Brooks was born in Medford:

“Mercy, d. Thomas [Thomas Jr., c.r.i.] and Mercy Jr., Sept. 3, 1763.”

Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston: NEHGS, 1907). Book scan, births (under Brooks) (Transcribed from the printed page.)

Her mother was Mercy (Tufts) Brooks; the Medford marriage record of Dec. 29, 1762, names Mercy Tufts and Thomas Brooks Jr. (Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (Boston: NEHGS, 1907). Book scan, marriages). She married Cotton Tufts Jr. of Weymouth in 1788 (intentions at Weymouth, Feb. 2; marriage at Medford, Mar. 6).

The Weymouth collection holds her diary for the year 1799 (Weymouth Public Libraries, Tufts / Smith / Adams Family Collection finding aid (Collection ID WCT002; prepared C. G. Swank, June 2016). Finding aid (PDF), Series II). She raised four children who lived to adulthood, including the library founders Quincy and Susannah.

“Mercy, b. Medford, wid. Cotton, d. Tho[ma]s Brooks and Mercy, old age, May 6, 1849, a. 85 y. 7 m. 4 d. [a. 86, G.R.14.]”

Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. II, Marriages and Deaths (Boston: NEHGS, 1910). Book scan, deaths (Transcribed from the printed page.)

Her Estate in the Probate Index

The Norfolk County probate index lists two Weymouth administrations under “Mercy” Tufts: one in 1849, case no. 18,848, and one in 1858, case no. 18,849 (Probate Index, Norfolk County, Massachusetts, 1793–1900, vol. 2 (1910), p. 1090. Internet Archive; see the clipping). The 1849 entry agrees with her death on May 6, 1849.

⚑ Unverified

The 1858 administration (no. 18,849) may be a later proceeding on her estate or the estate of another Mercy Tufts of Weymouth, possibly her daughter Mercy. The file has not been read. Note: Pending further work.
