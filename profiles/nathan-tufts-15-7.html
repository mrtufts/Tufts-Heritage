Nathan Tufts of Medford was born on October 5, 1741. He supported the war by lending toward the bounties paid to enlisting men in 1776; no military service is recorded for him in this entry. He died on June 12, 1784.

Summary drawn from the sources cited on this page.

Biography

Nathan Tufts of Medford supported the war with money rather than a musket, lending toward the bounties paid to enlisting men in 1776.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: 1741 – 1784

Born 5 Oct. 1741 in Medford; died 12 June 1784; lent money for bounty in 1776. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 47, family no. 15-7)
