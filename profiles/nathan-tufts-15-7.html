Biography

Nathan Tufts of Medford supported the war with money rather than a musket, lending toward the bounties paid to enlisting men in 1776.

Service Record

Town: Medford · Dates: 1741 – 1784

Born 5 Oct. 1741 in Medford; died 12 June 1784; lent money for bounty in 1776. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 120, family no. 15-7; owner-supplied extract; page scan not on file)
