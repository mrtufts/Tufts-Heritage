Biography

James Arthur Tufts, known to generations of Exeter alumni as “Tuffy,” was born at Alstead, New Hampshire, on April 26, 1855, the son of Timothy Tufts and Hannah Sophia (Kingsbury) Tufts. He entered Phillips Exeter Academy in 1872, graduated from Harvard in 1878, and returned that year to teach there (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, pp. 405–406, no. 583-2). Hurd, writing in 1886, calls him a professor at Exeter who had married Effie Locke of Arlington, Massachusetts, in 1878 and had a son and a daughter (Duane Hamilton Hurd, ed., History of Cheshire and Sullivan Counties, New Hampshire (Philadelphia: J. W. Lewis, 1886), Alstead sketch of Timothy Tufts, transcribed at Access Genealogy (printed page not confirmed)).

The family record says he became Odlin Professor of English in 1885 and Professor of English Literature in 1907, was secretary of the faculty from 1889, compiled the Academy’s general catalogue of 1783–1903, and served as secretary of the New England Alumni Association of Phillips Exeter from 1891 until his death. He sat in the New Hampshire Senate from 1919 to 1922, the last year as its president, and received honorary degrees from Dartmouth (1917), the University of New Hampshire (1920) and Boston University (1928) (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, pp. 405–406). He died at Exeter on November 21, 1938.

His eldest child, Effie Miriam, born November 27, 1879, died on November 2, 1903, after her dress caught fire in the yard of the family home; her mother was burned trying to save her (Thomas W. Tufts, Tufts Family Genealogy, post of 1 Dec. 2012). Letters of condolence from that November and December are described below.

Service Record

Town: Alstead and Exeter, N.H. · Dates: 26 Apr. 1855 – 21 Nov. 1938

Civil and academic service: Phillips Exeter faculty 1878 to about 1938; New Hampshire Senate 1919–1922 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, pp. 405–406). No military service is recorded for him in the family record.

Family Connection

Parents: Timothy Tufts of Alstead (1824–1901) and Hannah Sophia (Kingsbury) Tufts. Uncle: Hon. James Tufts (1829–1884). Grandfather: Jonas Tufts (1798–1879). Children by Effie Locke, as listed in the family record: Effie M., Irving E., Theodora, Delmont L., James A. and Helen (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 405).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Timothy Tufts of Alstead (1824–1901); uncle Hon. James Tufts (1829–1884); grandfather Jonas Tufts (1798–1879).

Spouse(s)

  • Effie Locke (25 Nov. 1853 – 29 Oct. 1931), adopted daughter of Benjamin Delmont and Sarah (Child) Locke of Arlington; married December 21, 1878, at Arlington (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, pp. 405–406).
  • Ruth Burrage Kilbourn (23 Feb. 1888 – 12 Mar. 1951), daughter of William Arthur and Abbie Fletcher (Goss) Kilbourn; married May 8, 1936, at Petersham; no issue (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 406).
