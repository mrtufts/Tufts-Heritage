Biography

Lucy Quincy, of Braintree, was the first wife of Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815). They were married at Weymouth on December 2, 1755: “TUFTS, Cotton, Dr., and Mrs. Lucy Quincey of Brantree, Dec. 2, 1755.” Vital Records of Weymouth, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850, vol. 2 (Boston, 1910), p. 199 (and p. 156 under QUINCEY). The same intention was entered at Braintree on November 1, 1755: “Doct. Cotton Tufts of Weymouth & Mrs Lucy Quincy of Braintree entered their Intention of Marriage.” Samuel A. Bates, ed., Records of the Town of Braintree, 1640 to 1793 (Randolph, Mass., 1886), p. 816, under the heading “1755.”

She died at Weymouth on October 30, 1785: “Lucy, w. Hon. Cotton Esq., Oct. 30, 1785. [[dup. Lucy Quincy, w. Cotton, M. D.] a. 56, G.R.14.]” Weymouth VR, vol. 2, p. 357. Age 56 on that date points to a birth about 1728–29.

Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 37–38, no. 11-4, identifies her as Lucy Quincy, daughter of Hon. John and Elizabeth (Norton) Quincy, born 1 December 1729 at Braintree, and the mother of one child, Cotton Jr. Bates, Records of the Town of Braintree, pp. 762–764 (the 1729 births), has no Lucy Quincy birth. The 1 December 1729 entry on p. 763 is “Sarah, the daughter of Samuel Belcher and Sarah his wife was born Decr. 1, 1729.” The exact birth date and parentage therefore remain as compiled in Kinsmen until a primary birth line is found in some other Braintree or church record.

Cotton married, second, Susanna Warner Tufts (1746–1832) on October 22, 1789, after Lucy’s death.

Family Connection

Husband: Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815). Child: Cotton Tufts Jr. (1757–1833). Father and mother (compiled): Hon. John Quincy and Elizabeth (Norton) Quincy (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 37).

Relatives with profiles on this site: husband Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815); son Cotton Tufts Jr. (1757–1833); Cotton’s second wife Susanna Warner Tufts (1746–1832); brother-in-law Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786); brother-in-law Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799); father-in-law Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747).

Spouse(s)

  • Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815), as his first wife. Married at Weymouth, December 2, 1755 (Weymouth VR, vol. 2, p. 199). He died at Weymouth, December 8, 1815, a. 84 (Weymouth VR, vol. 2, p. 357).
