Biography

Simon Tufts was born at Newburyport on March 7, 1769, the son of Samuel and Sarah: “Simon, s. Samuel and Sarah, Mar. 7, 1769.” Vital Records of Newburyport, Massachusetts, to the End of the Year 1849, vol. 1, Births (Salem, 1911), p. 399. His father was Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799), physician, druggist, and merchant, and his mother was Sarah (Moody) Tufts.

On July 29, 1797, Elizabeth Smith Shaw Peabody wrote to Abigail Adams from Atkinson that “Our worthy friend Mr Sam. Tufts drinks deep of the bitter Cup— He will soon be called to part with all his Children I fear— His Son, & daughter now lie at the point of death.” Founders Online, Elizabeth Smith Shaw Peabody to Abigail Adams, 29 July 1797. The letter does not name the son and daughter. The Adams Papers editorial note to that letter identifies them as Simon (b. 1769) and Sally, and gives their deaths as 8 and 15 August 1797, citing the Boston Columbian Centinel of 19 August, the Newburyport Political Gazette of 10 and 17 August, and the Newburyport birth volume already quoted.

Vital Records of Newburyport, Massachusetts, to the End of the Year 1849, vol. 2, Marriages and Deaths (Salem, 1911), p. 821: “Simon, s. Samuel and Sarah, Aug. 8, 1797.” The line does not say that he was single. Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 73, no. 33-4, calls him a merchant and says he died unmarried; that last point is not stated on the tan-book death line. The August 1797 newspaper notices cited by the Adams Papers editors have not been obtained as free page images. A compiled American Antiquarian Society index of Columbian Centinel obituaries lists “Tufts, Simon d. in Newburyport, aged 27 (C.C. Aug. 12, 1797) & (C.C. Aug. 19, 1797)” (Index of Obituaries in Massachusetts Centinel and Columbian Centinel, 1784–1840, vol. 5, p. 4580). Age 27 in August 1797 is consistent with the 7 March 1769 birth.

Service Record

Town: Newburyport, Mass. · Dates: 7 Mar. 1769 – 8 Aug. 1797

No military service has been found for him. He was a child during the Revolution and is noticed here as a son of Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport.

Family Connection

Parents: Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799) and Sarah (Moody) Tufts. Sister treated on this site: Sally Tufts of Newburyport (1772–1797). Other children of Samuel and Sarah recorded at Newburyport (vol. 1, p. 399) include Sarah, Sept. 21, 1763; Samuel, Aug. 30, 1765; Dolly, Mar. 22, 1767; and Polly, Feb. 4, 1775. Uncles: Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815) and Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786). Grandfather: Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Dr. Samuel Tufts of Newburyport (1735–1799); sister Sally Tufts of Newburyport (1772–1797); uncle Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815); uncle Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786); grandfather Dr. Simon Tufts (1700–1747).

Spouse(s)

No marriage has been found in the sources seen. Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 73, no. 33-4, says he died single; that statement has not been confirmed from the Newburyport marriage or death volumes.
