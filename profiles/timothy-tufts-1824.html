Biography

Timothy Tufts was born at Charlestown, New Hampshire, on May 29, 1824, the eldest child of Jonas Tufts and Sarah (Labaree) Tufts. Hurd’s county history says that he had a common-school and academy education and taught for several years, until 1844, when he went into trade as a clerk in Alstead village. In 1848 he formed a partnership with Oliver B. Campbell, and from 1851 he conducted the business alone. He kept a farm on road 28, belonged to the Congregational church, and was town treasurer for more than ten years (Duane Hamilton Hurd, ed., History of Cheshire and Sullivan Counties, New Hampshire (Philadelphia: J. W. Lewis, 1886), Alstead sketch of Timothy Tufts, transcribed at Access Genealogy (printed page not confirmed)).

The 1884–85 business directory lists him as “justice of the peace, dealer in general merchandise, and farmer 200” on Main Street, with a clerk in his store (Hamilton Child, Business Directory of Cheshire County, N.H., 1884–85, Alstead entries, transcribed at Family Findings). He died at Bellows Falls, Vermont, on December 17, 1901 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 165, no. 309-1).

On December 29, 1849, at Alstead, he married Hannah Sophia Phillette Kingsbury. Their son James Arthur Tufts became a professor at Phillips Exeter Academy. Timothy’s brother was Hon. James Tufts.

Family Connection

Parents: Jonas Tufts (1798–1879) and Sarah (Labaree) Tufts. Brother: Hon. James Tufts (1829–1884). Sisters: Sarah, Martha F., Clarissa J. and Susan. Children (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 405): Sophia (b. and d. May 1853); James Arthur (1855–1938); John T. (12 Mar. 1861 – 15 Feb. 1862); Sarah (14 Sept. 1863 – 24 Feb. 1864).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Jonas Tufts (1798–1879); brother Hon. James Tufts (1829–1884); son James Arthur Tufts (1855–1938).

Spouse(s)

  • Hannah Sophia Phillette Kingsbury (18 Dec. 1825 – 18 Apr. 1900), daughter of Joseph and Kezia (Kingsbury) Kingsbury; married December 29, 1849, at Alstead, and died there (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 165). Hurd gives her as “H. Sophia P., daughter of Joseph Kingsbury.”
