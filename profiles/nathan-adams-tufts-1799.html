Biography

Nathan Tufts, known as Nathan Adams Tufts, was born in Charlestown on January 16, 1799, a son of Deacon Amos Tufts. The family record describes him as a merchant and bank director. He married Mary Ann Lamson of Charlestown at Keene, New Hampshire, on September 11, 1822, and died in Charlestown on November 20, 1873 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 231, no. 128-7).

President of the Warren Institution for Savings

The Massachusetts Register for 1855 lists the officers of the Warren Institution for Savings, Charlestown: “Nathan A. Tufts, Pres.” (The Massachusetts Register for 1855, p. 151. Internet Archive). The Suffolk County history gives his presidency as 1850 to 1855 (Professional and Industrial History of Suffolk County, Massachusetts, vol. 2 (1894), p. 412. Internet Archive). His brother Joseph F. Tufts was its treasurer from 1851 to 1854, and his nephew George F. Tufts later held the same office. In the same year the city government of Charlestown included “N. A. Tufts” on the school committee for Ward 1, and an Amos Tufts on the common council (The Massachusetts Register for 1855, p. 253. Internet Archive). See also Banks and savings institutions.

Family Connection

Father: Deacon Amos Tufts (c. 1762–1839). Brothers included Joseph F. Tufts, William Tufts and Edward Tufts. Children: William Lamson (1824), Nathan Adams (1825–1826), Hannah Lamson (1828–1906), who married Nathan P. B. Curtiss, and Henry Adams Tufts (1831–1884), a merchant of New York (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 231; Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. II, p. 142–143).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father Deacon Amos Tufts; brother Joseph F. Tufts; brother William Tufts; brother Edward Tufts; nephew George F. Tufts.

Spouse(s)

  • Mary Ann Lamson (b. December 17, 1801, at Keene, N.H.; d. January 1, 1878, at Boston), daughter of William and Margery (Russell) Lamson of Charlestown; married September 11, 1822, at Keene (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 231).
