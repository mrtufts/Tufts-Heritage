Biography

Thomas Tufts was a son of Peter Tufts Jr. and Hannah Adams. The family record gives his birth as 22 March 1785, at Medford, and his death as 23 January 1844, at Utica, New York (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234–235, no. 129-6; Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 153. Page (Internet Archive)). Booth says that, of Peter and Hannah’s sons, Thomas and Aaron settled in New York State (Edward C. Booth, “The Tufts Family in Somerville,” Historic Leaves (Somerville Historical Society), vol. 1 (1902–03), p. 22. Text (Perseus)). Wyman names Thomas among the children of Peter 38, without dates (Thomas Bellows Wyman, The Genealogies and Estates of Charlestown, vol. II (1879), p. 965. Page (Internet Archive)).

The family record calls him a land agent, and names his wife as Sarah Barrows, with a marriage at Greenwich, Massachusetts, and children Sarah A., Andrew C., Caroline, Lucretia and Thomas B. (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234–235, no. 129-6).

Service Record

Town: Medford, Mass.; later Utica and LeRoy, N.Y. · Dates: 22 March 1785 – 23 January 1844

The family record states that during the War of 1812 he served New York as an ensign and paymaster of a light-infantry company (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 235). No muster roll has been seen in this pass.

Family Connection

Father: Peter Tufts Jr. (1753–1832). Mother: Hannah Adams (d. 1816). Brothers and sisters with profiles on this site: Peter Tufts the surveyor, Joel, Aaron, Sally (Tufts) Locke.

Relatives with profiles on this site: sister Sally (Tufts) Locke (1789–1871); brother Joel Tufts (1781–1852); father Peter Tufts Jr. (1753–1832); brother Peter Tufts the surveyor (1774–1825); brother Aaron Tufts (1793–1881).

Spouse(s)

  • Sarah Barrows, daughter of Daniel and Lydia (Tuttle) Barrows, of a marriage at Greenwich, Massachusetts. The family record gives her dates as 27 March 1785 (Mansfield, Conn.) – 15 November 1857 (LeRoy, N.Y.) (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234–235). The year of the marriage has not been confirmed.
