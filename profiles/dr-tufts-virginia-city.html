Biography

A printed bill form of the 1880s, made out at Virginia City, Nevada, is headed “To Drs. Packer & Tufts” and charges for medicine. The first name of Dr. Tufts is not on the sheet.

William Harvey King, History of Homœopathy and Its Institutions in America (New York, 1905), chapter XLI, “Homœopathy in Nevada,” by Thomas Lindsley Bradford, the list of “Homœopathic physicians in Nevada previous to 1886.” Text (homeoint.org) lists, among physicians in Nevada before 1886, “Tufts, J. E., Virginia City,” without a year of beginning practice, and “Packer, Fred H., Virginia City,” as beginning in 1871. That list is the authority used here for the initials J. E.; the billhead itself does not give them.
