Biography

Sarah Tufts was born on August 30, 1718. Her first husband, Thomas Backus of Canterbury, Connecticut, whom she married in 1740 at Haddam, Connecticut, died about a year later (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 28, no. 7-4). About 1747 she married Jonathan Whitcomb of Littleton, and the Littleton town records preserve the family:

“Jonathan m. about 1747 Sarah, b. Aug. 30 1718, dau. Peter Tufts of Charlestown, and 1745 took a deed of the house and 3 acres of land known as the residence of the late Peter Whitcomb. (Jonathan’s d. on pp. 152, 365.) Sarah d. at Shelburne Falls at the residence of her dau. Martha.”

Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 503. Internet Archive

Jonathan Whitcomb was a Littleton selectman from 1777 to 1782 and died in 1790 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 28). Their children were Sarah, who married Josiah Willis Seaver and lived at Moretown, Vermont; Martha, who married Jonas Rice in 1776 and lived at Shelburne; Jonathan; Peter; Lydia; and Dinah, who married Simeon Fiske of Shelburne in 1784 (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 504. Internet Archive).

Her Son Peter at Bunker Hill

Her son Peter Whitcomb, born at Littleton on December 24, 1756 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 28), “d. June 17 1775, killed at Battle of Bunker Hill” (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 504. Internet Archive). The family record adds that he had walked from Littleton to Charlestown that day to visit his Tufts relations and was struck by a stray bullet (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 51).

The Name Carried On

The family kept her maiden name in use. Her grandson Tufts Whitcomb, “fourth son of Jonathan Whitcomb and Sarah his Wife,” was born November 22, 1795, and died September 29, 1799 (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 383. Internet Archive), and the church record of burials notes “Tufts Whitcomb Son of Jonathan Whitcomb & Wife Aged about four years” (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 346. Internet Archive). That Jonathan was her son, who married Sarah Baker (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 504. Internet Archive).

Service Record

Town: Malden, Mass.; Littleton, Mass.; died at Shelburne Falls, Mass. · Dates: 30 August 1718 – 20 February 1801

No service record applies to her. Her son Peter Whitcomb was killed at Bunker Hill on June 17, 1775 (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 504. Internet Archive).

Family Connection

Father: Peter Tufts. The family record identifies him as Peter Tufts Jr. (1678–1756), son of Capt. Peter Tufts (c. 1648–1721) and Elizabeth (Lynde) Tufts, who married Deborah Lawrence of Reading in 1708 and later moved to Ashford (now Eastford), Connecticut (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 16, 28, no. 2-3; 7-4). Children: Sarah, Martha, Jonathan, Peter (1756–1775), Lydia and Dinah Whitcomb (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 504. Internet Archive).

Relatives with profiles on this site: granddaughter Sarah (Tufts) Whitcomb (1718–1801).

Spouse(s)

  • Thomas Backus (b. 1713 at Canterbury, Conn.; d. about 1741), son of Timothy and Sarah (Post) Backus; married 1740 at Haddam, Conn. No children (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 28).
  • Jonathan Whitcomb (1717–1790), lieutenant and farmer of Littleton, son of Jonathan and Deliverance (Nutting) Whitcomb; married about 1747 (Records of Littleton, Massachusetts (1900), p. 503. Internet Archive; Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 28).
