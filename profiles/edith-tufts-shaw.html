Biography

Edith Tufts was a daughter of Albert C. Tufts, the Sacramento druggist. A Social Security-derived index gives her birth as 7 October 1878, at Sacramento, under the married name Edith Tufts Shaw. Sacramento city directories place “Tufts Edith Miss” at the family addresses in the late 1890s, and in 1902 list her as a teacher at the Union Primary School, residing at 817 18th Street. She is named with her father, Mrs. Eva Nelson Tufts, and her sister Jessie May Tufts in the historic-churches index of St. Paul’s Episcopal Church, Sacramento (1852–1910). The directory record is gathered on her father’s profile and on the 19th Century page.
