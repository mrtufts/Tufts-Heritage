Biography

Joel Tufts was a son of Peter Tufts Jr. and Hannah Adams. The family record gives his birth as 22 December 1781, at Medford, and his death as 4 May 1852, at Peterborough, New Hampshire (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234, no. 129-4). Booth, writing of the eleven children of Peter and Hannah, says that Peter (the surveyor) and Joel stayed in Somerville, while Thomas and Aaron settled in New York State (Edward C. Booth, “The Tufts Family in Somerville,” Historic Leaves (Somerville Historical Society), vol. 1 (1902–03), p. 22. Text (Perseus)).

He married, first, on 13 January 1805 at Menotomy (now Arlington), Sarah Butterfield, daughter of William and Deborah (Locke) Butterfield; she died at West Cambridge on 14 November 1821. Their children, as the family record lists them, were Joel, Amanda, Sarah, Rebecca and Hannah. He married, second, on 23 October 1828 at Peterborough, Rebecca Brackett; they had no children (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234, no. 129-4).

⚑ Unverified

Wyman’s list of the children of Peter 38 (p. 965) names Peter, Hannah, Anna, Thomas, Rebecca, Sally, Aaron, Eliza and Lucretia, but not Joel. Booth and the family record both include him. Booth’s statement that Joel stayed in Somerville describes his earlier residence; the family record places his death at Peterborough. Note: Pending further work.

Family Connection

Father: Peter Tufts Jr. (1753–1832). Mother: Hannah Adams (d. 1816). Brothers and sisters with profiles on this site: Peter Tufts the surveyor, Thomas, Aaron, Sally (Tufts) Locke.

Relatives with profiles on this site: sister Sally (Tufts) Locke (1789–1871); father Peter Tufts Jr. (1753–1832); brother Peter Tufts the surveyor (1774–1825); brother Thomas Tufts (1785–1844); brother Aaron Tufts (1793–1881).

Spouse(s)

  • Sarah Butterfield (7 March 1784 – 14 November 1821), daughter of William and Deborah (Locke) Butterfield. Married 13 January 1805, at Menotomy (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234).
  • Rebecca Brackett (6 July 1801 – 4 July 1880), daughter of Josiah and Mary (Stuart) Brackett. Married 23 October 1828, at Peterborough, N.H. No children. She later married Norton Hunt, 14 March 1861 (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 234).
