Biography

Rowland Cotton Tufts was a son of Rev. Joshua Tufts and Abigail (Ellery) Tufts. The family record gives his birth as 1 March 1763, at Fort Cumberland, Nova Scotia (now Fort Beauséjour), and his death as 10 June 1768, at Halifax, a child (Tufts Kinsmen, Vol. I, p. 69, no. 28-13). He is named with his brothers and sisters on his father’s profile. The given names recall the Cotton family, from which several New England Tuftses also descend.
