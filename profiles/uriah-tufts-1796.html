Biography

The son of the blacksmith Uriah Tufts, he chose the finer trade of cabinetmaking and kept a shop in Boston for many years while living across the river in Charlestown.

“Uriah (2), son of Uriah (1) Tufts, was born in Boston, November 13, 1796, and died August 14, 1860. He was educated in the district schools, learned the trade of cabinet maker, and for many years was in business in Boston, residing in Charlestown.”

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

His first wife, Deborah Weston Thomas, died in 1829, leaving young children; of their five, only William (1820–1893) is known to have lived to adulthood. William was put to work at nine, the year his mother died (Cutter, pp. 1199–1200).
