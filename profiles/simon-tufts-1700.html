Biography

“Dr. Simon Tufts, son of Peter, born in Medford, Jan. 31, 1700, died here, Jan. 31, 1747. He graduated at Harvard College in 1724.”

Charles Brooks, History of the Town of Medford (1855), p. 302–303. Text (Perseus)

Brooks says he was called “the first physician of Medford,” and that the town records call him doctor in May 1724. His obituary, which Brooks reprints, says he “took his degrees at Harvard College in the years 1724 and 1727” and was the youngest son of Captain Peter Tufts by his second wife, a daughter of the Rev. Seaborn Cotton. His sons Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786) and Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815) both became physicians. See Harvard and the Drs. Simon Tufts house.

Service Record

Town: Medford, Mass. · Dates: 1700–1747

Justice of the peace from 1733 (obituary quoted by Brooks, p. 303). His service was medical and civil; he died before the Revolution.

Family Connection

Son of Capt. Peter Tufts and his second wife, Mercy Cotton (Brooks, p. 303). Father of the two doctors Simon and Cotton.

Relatives with profiles on this site: grandfather Peter Tufts the immigrant; son Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786); son Dr. Cotton Tufts (1732–1815); grandson Turell Tufts; grandson Simon Tufts (1750–1801); father Capt. Peter Tufts; sister Sarah Tufts Hinckley.

Justice, Physician to the College, Death and Burial

“He was honored with three commissions,—one for the peace, in the year 1733; another for a special justice, in 1741; and a third for justice of the quorum, 1743”

Charles Brooks, History of the Town of Medford (1855), p. 303. Text (Perseus), quoting his obituary of Feb. 5, 1747

“He was often called to visit the sick at Harvard College; and, though not rich himself, never demanded fees except from rich students.”

Charles Brooks, History of the Town of Medford (1855), p. 303. Text (Perseus)

“On the 31st of January, died here, of a convulsive asthma, and this day was decently buried, Simon Tufts, Esq., having just completed his forty-seventh year.”

Charles Brooks, History of the Town of Medford (1855), p. 303. Text (Perseus), quoting the obituary dated “Medford, Feb. 5, 1747”

The Medford vital records give his death as Jan. 31, 1746/7, “a. 47 ‘to a day’”; he was born Jan. 31, 1700 (Brooks, p. 302). He died on his 47th birthday.

Burial: Salem Street Burying Ground, Medford, plot 35. Find a Grave memorial 26903835 (user-contributed). The same plot holds his son Dr. Simon Tufts (1727–1786), memorial 26903836.
