Biography

Uriah Tufts, born in Medford, became a Boston blacksmith whose family remembered him as a smith on the frigate Constitution.

“Uriah, s. Will[ia]m 4th and Susanah, Jan. 29, 1768.”

Vital Records of Medford, Massachusetts, to the Year 1850 (1907), p. 153. Page (Internet Archive)

“He was a blacksmith by trade, and for many years had a smithy on Constitution Wharf, Boston. He forged and drove the first bolts that were used in the construction of the famous frigate “Constitution.””

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

In 1800 he was admitted to the Massachusetts Charitable Mechanic Association, the society of Boston master craftsmen founded in 1795:

“Uriah Tufts, Blacksmith.”

Joseph T. Buckingham, Annals of the Massachusetts Charitable Mechanic Association (1853), p. 67. Page (Internet Archive), in the list of those admitted to membership in 1800

“A pitcher which he wrought in 1800 is owned by his granddaughter. It bears a significant coat-of-arms, and the motto: “By hammer and hand, all arts do stand.””

Cutter, Genealogical and Personal Memoirs, vol. II, p. 1199. Page (Internet Archive) (Scanned text; obvious scanning errors corrected.)

Cutter records that he died in Charlestown on October 22, 1837, and was buried in Malden.

Service Record

Town: Medford, Boston and Charlestown, Mass. · Dates: 1768–1837

Civil: member of the Massachusetts Charitable Mechanic Association from 1800 (Buckingham, p. 67). No military service found.

Family Connection

Father: William Tufts Jr. (1736–1782), “William 4th” in the Medford records. Mother: Susannah. Brother: Nathan Tufts Jr.. Grandfather: Lieut. William Tufts.

Children by Sarah Harris: Sarah; Elizabeth Lewis (b. March 10, 1792); William; and Uriah (1796–1860). By Mary Phillips: Mary (b. 1798), who married in Malden on January 29, 1818, her cousin William Tufts of Milton (Cutter, p. 1199).

Relatives with profiles on this site: father William Tufts Jr.; brother Nathan Tufts Jr.; grandfather Lieut. William Tufts; son Uriah Tufts (1796–1860); grandson William Tufts (1820–1893).

Spouse(s)

  • Sarah Harris, born in Watertown May 16, 1764. Married July 8, 1789. Died September 11, 1797.
  • Mary Phillips, born in Malden. Married November 30, 1797. Died in Charlestown, September 15, 1853 (Cutter, p. 1199).
