Letters, deeds, billheads and other single documents that have come to light as auction or dealer lots, each credited to the seller or auction house and linked to the person’s page. Photographs remain the property of the houses that made them; they are shown here with credit, for research. Identifications that have not been confirmed are flagged on the profiles.
