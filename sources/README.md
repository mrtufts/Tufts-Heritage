# Approved Primary Sources
- Tufts Kinsmen, Volumes I and II
- Daughters of the American Revolution (DAR) records
- *Massachusetts Soldiers and Sailors of the Revolutionary War*
- Find A Grave
- Town records of Charlestown and Medford, Massachusetts
- Paul Revere's 1798 letter to Jeremy Belknap
- Deposition of Paul Revere (Massachusetts Historical Society)

Anything not supported by these sources must be clearly flagged as unverified.
