Winter Hill

“At the outbreak of the Revolution, Peter Tufts lived at the top of Winter Hill in a little old house, which stood until a few years ago. Here he was soundly sleeping when Paul Revere came thundering up to the door, calling on the people to rouse and arm themselves, for the Regulars were coming.”

Alvin Lincoln Jones, Under Colonial Roofs (1894), p. 129 · page scan

“The tablet rests upon the historic door stone of the Anne Adams Tufts homestead and was recently presented to the chapter.”

Jay Franklin Tufts, Tufts Family History 1633–1963 (secondary source), p. 28, quoting the Boston Journal, June 22, 1909, on the tablet unveiled in Paul Revere Park, Winter Hill, Somerville · page scan

Medford & Charlestown

“Lived in house occupied at one time by his cousin Daniel, opposite Powder House; through his influence his homestead lot was set off to Charlestown in 1811.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 52 (Peter Tufts Jr.) · page scan

“lived near Powder House, Somerville; land where his house stood set off from Medford to Charlestown, 1811.”

Helen Tilden Wild, Medford in the Revolution (1903), p. 50 (Daniel Tufts) · page scan

Later Homes

  • Weymouth, Mass.: Dr. Cotton Tufts and the Tufts Library (19th Century)
  • Walnut Hill, Somerville and Medford: Charles Tufts's gift for Tufts College (19th Century)
  • Mystic, Conn.: Caleb Tufts, per Wild p. 50 (profile)
  • Sacramento, Calif.: Albert C. Tufts, druggist (19th Century)
