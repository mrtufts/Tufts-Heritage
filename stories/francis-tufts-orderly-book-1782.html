The Adjutant’s Book, Spring 1782

In the spring of 1782, Lieut. Francis Tufts was adjutant of Col. Michael Jackson’s 8th Massachusetts Regiment, then with the Continental Army in the Hudson Highlands. An adjutant’s orderly book was the daily register of general, brigade, garrison, and regimental orders. This volume, kept at West Point and the neighboring posts, runs from 7 April to 30 May 1782. Tufts signed the inside cover, “Francis Tufts Lieut. & Adj.t / April 7th 1782.”

The book now at the Huntington Library (mssHM 719 vol. 13) is one of a longer series in his hand. The immediately preceding volume, 10 January to 8 April 1782, is at the Library of Congress (MMC-1239, microfilm 16,806; catalog description). Later volumes from 1782 and 1783 are also at the Huntington (mssHM 719 vol. 14, vol. 15, vol. 16, and a clothing and pay book, mssHM 635). Others are at the Massachusetts Historical Society (Ms. N-1687, microfilm P-394) and the Maine Historical Society (Coll. 1507; vol. 1 and vol. 2 on the Maine Memory Network).

The transcription below follows the wording, spelling, abbreviations, and lineation of the manuscript. Words written above the line are placed in the text with a caret (for example, Head Quarters^new). Unclear readings are marked [?]. Each heading links to the corresponding page image in the Huntington Digital Library. The images themselves are not reproduced here.
